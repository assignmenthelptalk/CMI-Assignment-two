## What CMI Unit 311 covers

According to the CMI Level 3 syllabus (October 2024), Unit 311 is worth 6 credits, with 30 guided learning hours and 60 hours of total unit time. It has 4 learning outcomes and 15 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Purpose, objectives, deliverables, project planning, risk management, risk register, resource management, project life cycle, stakeholder analysis, team plans, project implementation and control.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the role and purpose of projects in an organisation

- 1.1 **Identify** the differences between projects and everyday activities
- 1.2 **Explain** the reasons organisations use projects
- 1.3 **Discuss** types of projects in an organisation

### Learning outcome 2: Understand how projects are developed

- 2.1 **Explain** stages of the project lifecycle
- 2.2 **Explain** the information required in the development of a project
- 2.3 **Identify** the reasons for engaging stakeholders in project development
- 2.4 **Produce** a project plan

### Learning outcome 3: Know how to deliver a project

- 3.1 **Identify** roles involved in the delivery of a project
- 3.2 **Discuss** factors affecting the allocation of project activities to individuals
- 3.3 **Compare** methods for communicating project activities to individuals
- 3.4 **Outline** how to manage risks to the successful delivery of a project

### Learning outcome 4: Know how to monitor progress to ensure successful project delivery

- 4.1 **Outline** how to monitor progress against a project plan
- 4.2 **Discuss** tools that can be used for monitoring progress of a project
- 4.3 **Determine** how to take corrective actions to ensure successful project delivery
- 4.4 **Assess** how completed projects can be evaluated

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 311 are:

- **Identify** (1.1, 2.3, 3.1): name or pick out the relevant items and say why they matter.
- **Explain** (1.2, 2.1, 2.2): say how and why, not just what.
- **Discuss** (1.3, 3.2, 4.2): set out the different arguments or viewpoints and reach a reasoned position.
- **Produce** (2.4): write or prepare the finished item.
- **Compare** (3.3): set two or more things side by side and say how they are alike and different.
- **Outline** (3.4, 4.1): give the main points briefly.
- **Determine** (4.3): work out and state the answer or choice, with reasons.
- **Assess** (4.4): weigh up the importance, extent or effect of something and make a judgement.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## Models and sources the indicative content suggests

The indicative content for Unit 311 points to the following. They are examples, not a required list, but they show what the criteria expect you to draw on:

- Project life cycle: initiating, planning, implementing, monitoring and controlling, closing, evaluating
- Gantt charts, issue logs and project management software, as monitoring tools (AC4.2)
- Risk rating, risk matrices and a risk register (AC3.4)
- Shannon and Weaver (1948) and Schramm, for communicating project activities (AC3.3)

## How to approach a Unit 311 assignment

- AC2.4 is a doing criterion: produce a project plan. Include tasks, timescales, milestones, resources and risks, and make sure it matches the lifecycle stages you explain in AC2.1.
- Use the same project through LO3 and LO4 so the roles (AC3.1), risks (AC3.4) and monitoring tools (AC4.2) all refer to it.
- AC4.3 asks you to determine corrective actions. Pick a realistic problem and show the action, such as reallocating resources or adjusting timescales.

## Where Unit 311 assignments go wrong

- Only describing the lifecycle in AC2.1. The criterion is to explain what happens at each stage.
- Producing a plan with no risks, even though the same unit asks how to manage them (AC3.4).
- Covering how to evaluate a completed project (AC4.4) by listing time and cost alone. The indicative content also names quality, outcomes and resources.

## How CMI suggests Unit 311 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write an account or assignment on the principles of contributing to the delivery a project within your own organisation
- Respond to a scenario based on how you would contribute to the delivery of a project in an organisation
- Write a report based on contributing to the delivery of a project
- Draw upon your own experience of contributing to the delivery of a project within your own organisation

CMI Level 3 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 2,000 to 2,500 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.

## CMI Unit 311 FAQ

**Do I need to submit a real project plan for Unit 311?**

AC2.4 asks you to produce a project plan, so a plan is evidence you need. Your centre's brief says whether it should be for a real or a scenario project.

**Which project methodology should I use?**

The syllabus does not name one. It points to the project life cycle and common tools, so use the approach your organisation uses.
