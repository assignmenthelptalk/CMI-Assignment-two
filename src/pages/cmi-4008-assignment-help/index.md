## What CMI Unit 4008 covers

According to the CMI Level 4 syllabus (July 2026), Unit 4008 is worth 7 credits, with 30 guided learning hours and 70 hours of total unit time. It has 4 learning outcomes and 10 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Equality legislation, Valuing diversity, Protected characteristics, Inclusive practices, Employee rights, Team dynamics, 360-degree feedback, Improvement plans, Assertive behaviour, Human resources, External support, Conflict management.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand legislation, regulations, policies, and codes of practice relevant to equality and valuing of diversity

- 1.1 **Describe** the need to acknowledge and recognise individuals' background and beliefs
- 1.2 **Discuss** the need to respect diversity, value people as individuals and not discriminate against individuals
- 1.3 **Describe** the process of providing individuals with the information needed for them to make informed decisions about exercising their rights

### Learning outcome 2: Understand how organisational and individual behaviour affects individuals and teams

- 2.1 **Describe** how individual behaviour can affect an individual and a team
- 2.2 **Describe** how organisational behaviour can affect an individual and a team
- 2.3 **Analyse** feedback from individuals on personal behaviour

### Learning outcome 3: Be able to use feedback to devise an improvement plan

- 3.1 **Devise** an improvement plan based upon received feedback

### Learning outcome 4: Understand how to encourage equality and diversity in others

- 4.1 **Identify** situations where others are not promoting equality and valuing diversity
- 4.2 **Provide** opportunities to help others to promote equality and value diversity
- 4.3 **Discuss** sources from which to seek support when experiencing difficulty in understanding how to promote equality and value diversity

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 4008 are:

- **Describe** (1.1, 1.3, 2.1, 2.2): give a clear account of the subject, with an example from work.
- **Discuss** (1.2, 4.3): set out the different arguments or viewpoints and reach a reasoned position.
- **Analyse** (2.3): break the subject into parts and show how they relate, including causes and effects, with evidence.
- **Devise** (3.1): work out a method or plan that would suit the situation.
- **Identify** (4.1): name or pick out the relevant items and say why they matter.
- **Provide** (4.2): supply what is asked for, with evidence.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## Models and sources the indicative content suggests

The indicative content for Unit 4008 points to the following. They are examples, not a required list, but they show what the criteria expect you to draw on:

- Equality Act 2010 and its protected characteristics
- Your organisation's policies and codes of practice
- ACAS guidance (a common external source for good practice)

## How to approach a Unit 4008 assignment

- Start LO1 with what the Equality Act 2010 covers and the protected characteristics, then link to your organisation's own policy.
- In LO2, use personal examples where you can. The syllabus asks about positive behaviours (assertiveness, equal treatment) and negative ones (aggression, discrimination, bullying, harassment).
- AC3.1 is a "devise" criterion. Base the plan on real feedback, with milestones, activities such as training, and success criteria.

## Where Unit 4008 assignments go wrong

- Describing the law without saying how it shapes behaviour at work.
- Writing a plan in LO3 that is not tied to the feedback in AC2.3.
- Overlooking where to find support (AC4.3).

## How Unit 4008 is assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met and that learners may approach the assessment in several ways. It does not prescribe one format for this unit, so your centre's assessment brief sets the task. It also says you do not have to cover every point of the indicative content: choose well and show you understand each criterion.

CMI Level 4 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 3,000 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.
