## What CMI Unit 315 covers

According to the CMI Level 3 syllabus (October 2024), Unit 315 is worth 6 credits, with 24 guided learning hours and 60 hours of total unit time. It has 4 learning outcomes and 9 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Legal requirements, guidelines, policies, procedures, purpose, benefits, hazard, risk assessment, damage.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the purpose and benefits of health and safety in the workplace

- 1.1 **Explain** the purpose of health and safety in the workplace
- 1.2 **Explain** the benefits of health and safety

### Learning outcome 2: Understand legal requirements in relation to health and safety in the workplace

- 2.1 **Explain** the obligations health and safety legislation places on an organisation
- 2.2 **Outline** the supplementary legal requirements which apply to different industry sectors

### Learning outcome 3: Understand the risk assessment process

- 3.1 **Explain** the difference between a hazard and a risk
- 3.2 **Explain** how to conduct a work based risk assessment

### Learning outcome 4: Know how to respond effectively to a workplace health and safety emergency

- 4.1 **Identify** a range of workplace health and safety emergencies
- 4.2 **Explain** the immediate action required of by the manager in response to an emergency
- 4.3 **Summarise** the next steps in response to an emergency

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 315 are:

- **Explain** (1.1, 1.2, 2.1, 3.1, 3.2, 4.2): say how and why, not just what.
- **Outline** (2.2): give the main points briefly.
- **Identify** (4.1): name or pick out the relevant items and say why they matter.
- **Summarise** (4.3): give the key points in a short, accurate form.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## Models and sources the indicative content suggests

The indicative content for Unit 315 points to the following. They are examples, not a required list, but they show what the criteria expect you to draw on:

- Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 (HSWA)
- Working Time Directive 1998 and industry-specific regulation, for AC2.2
- Risk assessment process: identify hazards, decide who might be harmed and how, evaluate risks and decide on precautions
- Written policies such as evacuation, accident reporting and smoking policy, for AC2.1

## How to approach a Unit 315 assignment

- Use your own workplace for the risk assessment in AC3.2, and show the steps in order, from hazards to who might be harmed to the precautions you would decide on.
- Keep the hazard and risk definitions in AC3.1 tight and use one example to show the difference, such as a wet floor (hazard) and the chance someone slips on it (risk).
- For LO4 pick one emergency from AC4.1, state your immediate action as manager (AC4.2) and then the next steps (AC4.3), such as informing managers, ensuring a place of safety and debriefing.

## Where Unit 315 assignments go wrong

- Writing only about the Act in AC2.1. The criterion asks for the obligations the legislation places on an organisation, such as written policies and procedures.
- Explaining risk assessment in general terms in AC3.2 with no worked example.
- Mixing the immediate response (AC4.2) with the next steps (AC4.3). The criteria treat them separately.

## How CMI suggests Unit 315 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write a report based on the legal requirements for workplace health and safety and their purpose and benefits, and how these are presented in the form of organisational guidelines
- Work through a scenario of how you would manage the risk assessment process and/or a health and safety emergency
- Draw upon your own experience of managing work place health and safety
- Present work based evidence, including a completed work place health and safety risk assessment, accompanied by reports/reflective accounts to meet each assessment criteria
- Produce a guide outlining good practice on managing health and safety in the workplace

CMI Level 3 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 2,000 to 2,500 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.
