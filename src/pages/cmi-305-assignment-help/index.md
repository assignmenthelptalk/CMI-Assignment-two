## What CMI Unit 305 covers

According to the CMI Level 3 syllabus (October 2024), Unit 305 is worth 4 credits, with 20 guided learning hours and 40 hours of total unit time. It has 4 learning outcomes and 13 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Relationship, stakeholders, interpersonal, styles, channels, listening, verbal, written, barriers to communication, needs and interests, internal, external and connected stakeholders.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Know the purpose of building stakeholder relationships

- 1.1 **Identify** a range of stakeholder groups relevant to the manager
- 1.2 **Explain** how to assess the needs and expectations of stakeholder groups
- 1.3 **Assess** the benefits of building relationships with stakeholders

### Learning outcome 2: Understand different channels and types of communication used to build relationships with stakeholders

- 2.1 **Compare** the channels of communication used to build stakeholder relationships
- 2.2 **Discuss** types of verbal communication used with stakeholders
- 2.3 **Explain** how active listening is used to build stakeholder relationships
- 2.4 **Discuss** types of written communication used with stakeholders
- 2.5 **Discuss** how meetings can be led to develop and build effective stakeholder relationships

### Learning outcome 3: Know how to respond to barriers when communicating with stakeholders

- 3.1 **Explain** barriers to effective communication with stakeholders
- 3.2 **Discuss** ways of responding to barriers when communicating with stakeholders

### Learning outcome 4: Know how to use stakeholder communication

- 4.1 **Discuss** the importance of stakeholder feedback
- 4.2 **Compare** methods of gathering feedback from stakeholders
- 4.3 **Discuss** the use of stakeholder feedback

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 305 are:

- **Identify** (1.1): name or pick out the relevant items and say why they matter.
- **Explain** (1.2, 2.3, 3.1): say how and why, not just what.
- **Assess** (1.3): weigh up the importance, extent or effect of something and make a judgement.
- **Compare** (2.1, 4.2): set two or more things side by side and say how they are alike and different.
- **Discuss** (2.2, 2.4, 2.5, 3.2, 4.1, 4.3): set out the different arguments or viewpoints and reach a reasoned position.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## Models and sources the indicative content suggests

The indicative content for Unit 305 points to the following. They are examples, not a required list, but they show what the criteria expect you to draw on:

- Freeman, Stakeholder Theory (1984)
- Eden and Ackermann, Power versus Interest Grid (1998)
- Shannon and Weaver (1948) and Schramm (1954), communication models
- Goleman, Emotional Intelligence (1995), for responding to barriers

## How to approach a Unit 305 assignment

- Draw up one stakeholder map for your own area in AC1.1, separating internal and external groups, and reuse it through the unit.
- LO2 asks for comparing and discussing, not just describing. Take three channels for AC2.1 and weigh them against each other for a named stakeholder group.
- In LO4 show a feedback loop: why feedback matters (AC4.1), how you gather it (AC4.2) and what you do with it (AC4.3).

## Where Unit 305 assignments go wrong

- Listing stakeholders in AC1.1 with no link to the manager. The criterion says groups relevant to the manager.
- Writing about active listening in AC2.3 as a personal skill rather than something used to build the stakeholder relationship.
- Discussing written communication (AC2.4) and verbal communication (AC2.2) in the same paragraph. They are separate criteria.

## How CMI suggests Unit 305 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write a report to discuss the stakeholder relationships you might be responsible for and/or involved in in your area of responsibility
- Present work based evidence showing examples of the type and nature of stakeholder relationships encountered in the workplace
- Draw on your own experience of working with different stakeholders in your own area of responsibility. Reflective accounts may be used to meet assessment criteria
- For learners who are not in employment, the use of a scenario or case study could be used to support the application of knowledge in context
- Write a guide to highlight good practice on communicating with stakeholders in the workplace

CMI Level 3 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 2,000 to 2,500 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.
