## What CMI Unit 508 covers

According to the CMI Level 5 syllabus (June 2026), Unit 508 is worth 4 credits, with 16 guided learning hours and 40 hours of total unit time. It has 4 learning outcomes and 12 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Skills, talent, development, benefits, impact, organisational and legal frameworks, technology, strategy, innovative.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the benefits of developing a skilled and talented workforce

- 1.1 **Assess** the impact of workforce development on organisations
- 1.2 **Examine** the impact of workforce development on an organisation's external stakeholders
- 1.3 **Discuss** the benefits of workforce development for individuals and teams

### Learning outcome 2: Understand the factors which influence workforce development

- 2.1 **Examine** the impact of legal and organisational factors on workforce development
- 2.2 **Analyse** the role of the manager in workforce development
- 2.3 **Analyse** the challenges and barriers to workforce development
- 2.4 **Evaluate** methods for overcoming challenges and barriers to workforce development

### Learning outcome 3: Understand the scope of learning and development as part of a workforce development strategy

- 3.1 **Analyse** factors which influence the selection of learning and development activities
- 3.2 **Assess** the scope of learning and development activities in workforce development
- 3.3 **Evaluate** the use of technology in delivering workforce development

### Learning outcome 4: Know how to measure the impact of workforce development

- 4.1 **Examine** methods for measuring the impact of workforce development
- 4.2 **Analyse** methods to sustain the impact of workforce development

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 508 are:

- **Assess** (1.1, 3.2): weigh up the importance, extent or effect of something and make a judgement.
- **Examine** (1.2, 2.1, 4.1): look closely at the subject and set out what it is, how it works and what it affects.
- **Discuss** (1.3): set out the different arguments or viewpoints and reach a reasoned position.
- **Analyse** (2.2, 2.3, 3.1, 4.2): break the subject into parts and show how they relate, including causes and effects, with evidence.
- **Evaluate** (2.4, 3.3): weigh strengths against limitations with evidence and reach a supported judgement.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 508 assignment

- Frame the whole assignment around one workforce development strategy so the four learning outcomes read as one story.
- For AC4.1, use Kirkpatrick's four levels (named in the syllabus) and be explicit about what is measurable at each level in your organisation.
- AC3.3 asks you to evaluate technology in delivering development, so weigh benefits and limits instead of listing tools.

## Where Unit 508 assignments go wrong

- Stopping at the benefits in LO1 and never reaching measurement in LO4.
- Covering barriers (AC2.3) without evaluating the methods for overcoming them (AC2.4).
- Ignoring external stakeholders in AC1.2.

## How CMI suggests Unit 508 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write an account or assignment on the principles of developing a skilled and talented workforce
- The learner should draw upon your own experience either from their current work role, paid or voluntary
- If not in employment, learners can draw upon organisations with which they are familiar e.g. a past employer or as part of a case study/scenario

CMI Level 5 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. Each unit has a guideline word count with a 10% allowance above or below, so check the figure in your brief before you plan your sections.

## CMI Unit 508 FAQ

**How is Unit 508 different from Unit 507?**

Unit 507 is about coaching and mentoring specifically. Unit 508 covers workforce development as a strategy and includes formal and informal learning, technology and impact measurement.

Some CMI listings show it as "Principles of Developing a Skilled and Talented Work". The unit is about the workforce.
