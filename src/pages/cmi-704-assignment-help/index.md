## What CMI Unit 704 covers

According to the CMI Level 7 syllabus (August 2026), Unit 704 is worth 9 credits, with 30 guided learning hours and 90 hours of total unit time. It has 2 learning outcomes and 5 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Strategy, direction, factors, culture, structure, approaches, deliberate, emergent, planning, implementation, monitoring, success.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand how to develop strategy

- 1.1 **Critique** the factors which drive the development of organisational strategy
- 1.2 **Critically appraise** approaches for the development of strategy
- 1.3 **Discuss** the challenges of developing and leading organisational strategy

### Learning outcome 2: Know how to develop strategy

- 2.1 **Develop** an organisational strategy to achieve a business objective
- 2.2 **Recommend** an approach to implement and monitor the strategy to ensure its success

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 704 are:

- **Critique** (1.1): give a detailed analysis and assessment of the subject.
- **Critically appraise** (1.2): use a systematic process to find the strengths and weaknesses of information and judge how useful and valid it is.
- **Discuss** (1.3): give a detailed account that includes a range of views, including contrasting perspectives.
- **Develop** (2.1): elaborate and progress an idea from a starting point, building on the information given.
- **Recommend** (2.2): put forward proposals or alternatives, supported by a clear rationale.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## Models and sources the indicative content suggests

The indicative content for Unit 704 points to the following. They are examples, not a required list, but they show what the criteria expect you to draw on:

- Schein (1988), and Johnson and Scholes (2011), on organisational culture
- Porter, Value Chain Analysis and Five Forces Model (1980)
- Bass and Riggio, Transformational Leadership (2006)
- Deliberate, emergent, incremental and step-change approaches to strategy

## How to approach a Unit 704 assignment

- LO1 is analytical and LO2 is a build task. Choose one real business objective in AC2.1 and use LO1 to show why your strategic choices suit it.
- AC1.1 uses "critique", which in CMI's Annex 1 is a detailed analysis and assessment of something. Weigh internal factors (culture, structure, finance) against external ones, not just list them.
- AC2.2 has two jobs: an approach to implement and an approach to monitor. Cover both, for example how progress is measured and what happens when the strategy needs changing.

## Where Unit 704 assignments go wrong

- Producing a PESTLE and SWOT in AC1.1 without critiquing how each factor drives strategy.
- Setting out a strategy in AC2.1 without growth, retrenchment or partnership options being compared.
- Recommending implementation in AC2.2 with no monitoring, or monitoring with no criteria for success.

## How CMI suggests Unit 704 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write a report on approaches to developing organisational strategy
- Develop an organisational strategy to achieve a business goal
- Produce a reflective account to evidence their ability to meet each of the assessment criteria

CMI Level 7 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 4,000 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.

## CMI Unit 704 FAQ

**Does Unit 704 require a real organisation?**

The assessment criteria do not say the strategy must be for your own employer, and your centre sets the brief. A real organisation usually makes the evidence in AC1.1 and AC2.1 easier to find.

**How does Unit 704 relate to Unit 701?**

Unit 701 focuses on the strategic leader and their behaviours. Unit 704 focuses on how strategy itself is developed, implemented and monitored, so the two are often used together.
