## What CMI Unit 606 covers

According to the CMI Level 6 syllabus (July 2026), Unit 606 is worth 6 credits, with 20 guided learning hours and 60 hours of total unit time. It has 2 learning outcomes and 5 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Organisational strategy, operational strategy, functional strategy, approaches, impact, inter-relationship proposal, outcomes, success.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand approaches to developing organisational strategy

- 1.1 **Critically assess** the inter-relationship between organisational strategy and functional and operational strategies
- 1.2 **Evaluate** factors which influence the development of organisational strategy
- 1.3 **Critically appraise** approaches to developing organisational strategy

### Learning outcome 2: Know how to develop and lead operational strategy to achieve a strategic aim

- 2.1 **Critically discuss** a process to develop an operational strategy to achieve a strategic aim
- 2.2 **Recommend** approaches to leading operational strategy and achieving stakeholder buy-in

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 606 are:

- **Critically assess** (1.1): give a reasoned judgement on the arguments made by others and on the evidence behind them, checking the validity of your sources.
- **Evaluate** (1.2): weigh strengths and weaknesses and arguments for and against, then reach a reasoned judgement.
- **Critically appraise** (1.3): use a systematic process to find the strengths and weaknesses of information and judge how useful and valid it is.
- **Critically discuss** (2.1): discuss all aspects and effects of the topic and weigh the pros and cons critically.
- **Recommend** (2.2): put forward proposals or alternatives, supported by a clear rationale.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 606 assignment

- Use one organisation's strategy for LO1, then choose one strategic aim and build an operational strategy for it in LO2.
- AC1.1 asks for the inter-relationship between strategic, functional and operational levels, so show how direction at the top turns into resources, deliverables and timescales lower down.
- AC2.1 is built around a process. The indicative content points to gap analysis, resourcing and market-facing strategies; take each step in order.

## Where Unit 606 assignments go wrong

- Describing one strategy model in AC1.3. The criterion asks you to appraise approaches, so compare more than one.
- Listing influences on strategy in AC1.2 with no evaluation of which matter most in your organisation.
- Making recommendations in AC2.2 that do not say how you would gain stakeholder buy-in.

## How CMI suggests Unit 606 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Written account on the approaches to developing organisational strategies
- Respond to a management report on developing and leading operational strategy to achieve a strategic aim
- Present work-based evidence accompanied by reports/reflective accounts to meet each of the assessment criteria

CMI Level 6 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 4,000 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.

## CMI Unit 606 FAQ

**What is the difference between strategic and operational strategy here?**

The indicative content treats operational strategy as the plan that delivers a strategic aim in practice, including gap analysis, resourcing and stakeholder buy-in.

**Do I need to use Mintzberg?**

No. Deliberate and emergent strategies are cited as an example. You can use another recognised approach if you justify it.
