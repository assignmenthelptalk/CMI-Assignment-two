## What CMI Unit 4005 covers

According to the CMI Level 4 syllabus (July 2026), Unit 4005 is worth 7 credits, with 25 guided learning hours and 70 hours of total unit time. It has 3 learning outcomes and 9 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Report writing, Terms of reference, Reporting channels, Information gathering, Data integrity, Data analysis, Logical summaries, Recommendations, Decision-making, Audience analysis, Selection criteria, Report components.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the purpose of management reporting

- 1.1 **Determine** circumstances which could require a management report
- 1.2 **Compare** the methods of management reporting available to a manager
- 1.3 **Justify** a method of management reporting to achieve a management objective

### Learning outcome 2: Be able to construct a written management report

- 2.1 **Construct** the terms of reference for a report
- 2.2 **Identify** the component parts of a written management report
- 2.3 **Produce** conclusions and recommendations that meet the report objective or terms of reference

### Learning outcome 3: Understand the collection and analysis of data and information required for a written management report

- 3.1 **Distinguish** between data and information
- 3.2 **Create** criteria to select data and information
- 3.3 **Evaluate** methods to analyse data and information

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 4005 are:

- **Determine** (1.1): work out and state the answer or choice, with reasons.
- **Compare** (1.2): set two or more things side by side and say how they are alike and different.
- **Justify** (1.3): give reasons and evidence that support a decision or recommendation.
- **Construct** (2.1): build the item, such as terms of reference, from its parts.
- **Identify** (2.2): name or pick out the relevant items and say why they matter.
- **Produce** (2.3): write or prepare the finished item.
- **Distinguish** (3.1): show clearly what sets one thing apart from the other.
- **Create** (3.2): produce the plan or document itself, fit for the stated purpose.
- **Evaluate** (3.3): weigh strengths against limitations with evidence and reach a supported judgement.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 4005 assignment

- Choose a realistic management question, such as "Is our reward scheme aligned with our performance measures?", and let it carry through LO1, LO2 and LO3.
- AC2.1 asks for the terms of reference. Cover the question, responsibilities, timescales and milestones, audience and how you will know the objective has been met.
- AC3.1 to AC3.3 are about data. Distinguish data from information, set criteria for selecting it, and evaluate how you would analyse it.

## Where Unit 4005 assignments go wrong

- Writing a report instead of showing how reports are constructed. This unit is about the skill and the choices behind it.
- Using "data" and "information" interchangeably after defining the difference (AC3.1).
- Recommendations (AC2.3) that do not answer the report objective.

## How Unit 4005 is assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met and that learners may approach the assessment in several ways. It does not prescribe one format for this unit, so your centre's assessment brief sets the task. It also says you do not have to cover every point of the indicative content: choose well and show you understand each criterion.

CMI Level 4 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 3,000 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.
