## What CMI Unit 5001 covers

According to the CMI Level 4 syllabus (July 2026), Unit 5001 (regulatory unit number F/504/9024) is at RQF level 5 and is worth 6 credits, with 20 guided learning hours and 60 hours of total unit time. It has 4 learning outcomes and 14 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Self-development, Gap analysis, Skills assessment, Development plan, Measurable goals, Business case, Resource planning, Return on investment, Staff welfare, Occupational health, Safety regulations, Compliance records.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Be able to assess and plan for personal professional development

- 1.1 **Identify** the importance of continual self-development in achieving organisational objectives
- 1.2 **Assess** current skills and competencies against defined role requirements and organisational objectives
- 1.3 **Identify** development opportunities to meet current and future defined needs
- 1.4 **Construct** a personal development plan with achievable but challenging goals

### Learning outcome 2: Be able to plan the resources required for personal professional development

- 2.1 **Identify** the resources required to support the personal development plan
- 2.2 **Develop** a business case to secure the resources to support the personal development plan

### Learning outcome 3: Be able to implement and evaluate the personal development plan

- 3.1 **Discuss** the processes required to implement the personal development plan
- 3.2 **Evaluate** the impact of the personal development plan on the achievement of defined role requirements and organisational objectives
- 3.3 **Review** and update the personal development plan

### Learning outcome 4: Be able to support and promote staff welfare

- 4.1 **Discuss** the relationship between staff welfare and organisational objectives
- 4.2 **Explain** the process for assessing staff welfare
- 4.3 **Explain** the actions to be taken by the manager in dealing with a staff welfare issue
- 4.4 **Describe** how to communicate responsibilities for staff welfare to the team
- 4.5 **Discuss** records that may be maintained to demonstrate that staff welfare is supported

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 5001 are:

- **Identify** (1.1, 1.3, 2.1): name or pick out the relevant items and say why they matter.
- **Assess** (1.2): weigh up the importance, extent or effect of something and make a judgement.
- **Construct** (1.4): build the item, such as a plan, from its parts.
- **Develop** (2.2): produce a plan or approach that would work for a stated need.
- **Discuss** (3.1, 4.1, 4.5): set out the different arguments or viewpoints and reach a reasoned position.
- **Evaluate** (3.2): weigh strengths against limitations with evidence and reach a supported judgement.
- **Review** (3.3): look back at what happened and say how well it went and why.
- **Explain** (4.2, 4.3): say how and why, not just what.
- **Describe** (4.4): give a clear account of the subject, with an example from work.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## Models and sources the indicative content suggests

The indicative content for Unit 5001 points to the following. They are examples, not a required list, but they show what the criteria expect you to draw on:

- Pedler, Burgoyne and Boydell (2007), Mullins (2013) and Torrington et al (2008), on the importance of personal development
- Torrington, Hall and Taylor (2008), for the business case in AC2.2
- The development cycle, Kolb, and learning styles (VAK, or Honey and Mumford), for development opportunities
- SMART objectives, performance indicators and return on investment (ROI)
- COSHH Regulations 2002, risk assessment and the Shannon and Weaver communications model, in the staff welfare outcome

## How to approach a Unit 5001 assignment

- Use your own role and organisation throughout. The indicative content for AC1.1 suggests answering with reference to your own development.
- AC1.4 asks for a personal development plan. The syllabus says it can be a chart or a narrative and, as a minimum, should include key tasks, SMART objectives, performance indicators, dates, milestones and review dates.
- AC2.2 is a business case for the resources in AC2.1. It can show timescales and possible payback, and a well-developed answer might show how you would calculate the return on investment.
- AC3.1 to AC3.3 follow the plan through: how it is put into action, what difference it makes, and how and with whom you review and update it.
- LO4 can become a very large section. The syllabus suggests limiting it to staff welfare issues you have some experience with, so you stay within the word count.

## Where Unit 5001 assignments go wrong

- Describing the implementation of the plan when AC3.1 asks you to discuss it. The indicative content says this is more than a mere description.
- Treating staff welfare as health and safety only. The indicative content says it is more than that and could include occupational health and work-life balance.
- A development plan (AC1.4) that is not linked back to the gaps found in AC1.2.
- A business case (AC2.2) for resources that AC2.1 did not identify.

## How Unit 5001 is assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met and that learners may approach the assessment in several ways. It does not prescribe one format for this unit, so your centre's assessment brief sets the task. It also says you do not have to cover every point of the indicative content: choose well and show you understand each criterion.

CMI Level 4 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 3,000 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.
