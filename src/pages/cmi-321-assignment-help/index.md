## What CMI Unit 321 covers

According to the CMI Level 3 syllabus (October 2024), Unit 321 is worth 5 credits, with 20 guided learning hours and 50 hours of total unit time. It has 4 learning outcomes and 12 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Personal and professional development, benefits, organisation, support, opportunities, legal requirements, planning, monitor.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the benefits of personal and professional development

- 1.1 **Identify** the differences and similarities between personal and professional development
- 1.2 **Explain** the benefits of personal and professional development for the individual
- 1.3 **Identify** the benefits to an organisation of individuals undertaking personal and professional development

### Learning outcome 2: Understand how personal and professional development is informed

- 2.1 **Explain** how organisations support personal and professional development
- 2.2 **Identify** how professional bodies/associations support and stipulate requirements for personal and professional development
- 2.3 **Summarise** the implications of legal requirements on personal and professional development

### Learning outcome 3: Know how to identify opportunities for personal and professional development

- 3.1 **Compare** different methods for undertaking personal and professional development
- 3.2 **Explain** the resource implications of different methods of personal and professional development
- 3.3 **Explain** how to choose the most appropriate method for personal and professional development

### Learning outcome 4: Know how to create and monitor a personal and professional development plan

- 4.1 **Assess** current skills and competencies against role requirements and organisational objectives using recognised tools and techniques
- 4.2 **Create** a personal and professional development plan to meet agreed objectives
- 4.3 **Explain** how the personal and professional development plan will be monitored

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 321 are:

- **Identify** (1.1, 1.3, 2.2): name or pick out the relevant items and say why they matter.
- **Explain** (1.2, 2.1, 3.2, 3.3, 4.3): say how and why, not just what.
- **Summarise** (2.3): give the key points in a short, accurate form.
- **Compare** (3.1): set two or more things side by side and say how they are alike and different.
- **Assess** (4.1): weigh up the importance, extent or effect of something and make a judgement.
- **Create** (4.2): produce the plan or document itself, fit for the stated purpose.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## Models and sources the indicative content suggests

The indicative content for Unit 321 points to the following. They are examples, not a required list, but they show what the criteria expect you to draw on:

## How to approach a Unit 321 assignment

- Do the self-assessment first. AC4.1 asks you to assess your current skills against role requirements and organisational objectives with recognised tools, and the rest of the unit builds on it.
- AC4.2 asks you to create the plan itself, so include SMART objectives across short, medium and long term, and say how you will monitor it (AC4.3).
- For AC2.2 name a real professional body in your field and say what it requires or supports.

## Where Unit 321 assignments go wrong

- Treating personal and professional development as identical in AC1.1. The criterion asks for differences and similarities.
- Choosing methods in AC3.3 without linking them to your needs, learning style, time and finance.
- Describing a plan but not how it will be monitored through feedback and reflection (AC4.3).

## How CMI suggests Unit 321 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Draw on your own experience from your current work role, paid or voluntary. The plan is not tied to a particular role and can include planning for a future role
- If you are not in employment, draw on an organisation you know, such as a past employer, or on a scenario or plan for a future role
- If your role has no professional body or association, use one you have researched as part of an exercise or scenario
- Present work-based evidence with reports or reflective accounts to meet each criterion

CMI Level 3 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 2,000 to 2,500 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.

## CMI Unit 321 FAQ

**Is Unit 321 the same as Unit 307?**

No. Unit 307 is about developing other people and teams. Unit 321 is about your own development.

**Does Unit 321 need a real development plan?**

AC4.2 asks you to create a plan to meet agreed objectives, so a plan is part of the evidence. Your centre confirms the format.
