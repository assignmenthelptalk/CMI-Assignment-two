## What CMI Unit 313 covers

According to the CMI Level 3 syllabus (October 2024), Unit 313 is worth 5 credits, with 19 guided learning hours and 50 hours of total unit time. It has 5 learning outcomes and 14 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Good practice, benefits, challenges, examples, impact, motivation, sharing, improvement, innovation, change.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Know the benefits of good practice

- 1.1 **Compare** good working practice against standard working practice
- 1.2 **Explain** how good practice can benefit an organisation
- 1.3 **Explain** the positive impact good practice can have on customers
- 1.4 **Outline** the potential rewards to individuals and teams who deliver good practice

### Learning outcome 2: Be able to identify good practice

- 2.1 **Identify** examples of good practice within workplace settings
- 2.2 **Assess** the factors that contribute to good practice

### Learning outcome 3: Know how to develop good practice

- 3.1 **Explain** how good practice can be developed
- 3.2 **Examine** potential barriers and threats to the development of good practice
- 3.3 **Explain** how barriers and threats to good practice can be overcome

### Learning outcome 4: Understand how good practice can be implemented, maintained and improved

- 4.1 **Outline** approaches to implementing good practice
- 4.2 **Explain** how good practice can be maintained
- 4.3 **Explain** the consequences of not maintaining good practice

### Learning outcome 5: Know how to share good practice with stakeholders

- 5.1 **Outline** approaches to share good practice with stakeholders
- 5.2 **Identify** legal and organisational requirements which impact on the ability to share good practice

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 313 are:

- **Compare** (1.1): set two or more things side by side and say how they are alike and different.
- **Explain** (1.2, 1.3, 3.1, 3.3, 4.2, 4.3): say how and why, not just what.
- **Outline** (1.4, 4.1, 5.1): give the main points briefly.
- **Identify** (2.1, 5.2): name or pick out the relevant items and say why they matter.
- **Assess** (2.2): weigh up the importance, extent or effect of something and make a judgement.
- **Examine** (3.2): look closely at the subject and set out what it is, how it works and what it affects.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## Models and sources the indicative content suggests

The indicative content for Unit 313 points to the following. They are examples, not a required list, but they show what the criteria expect you to draw on:

- Benchmarking, quality assurance and review, for maintaining good practice (AC4.2)
- Standard working practice, as the baseline in AC1.1
- Written reports, newsletters, good practice guides and web content, for sharing (AC5.1)

## How to approach a Unit 313 assignment

- Choose one example of good practice from a workplace setting for AC2.1 and use it as your case through the unit, from benefits to sharing.
- AC1.1 is a comparison, so set the good practice you picked against the standard working practice stated in contracts and agreements.
- In LO5 cover both sides of sharing: the approaches you could use (AC5.1) and the legal and organisational requirements, such as confidentiality, that limit it (AC5.2).

## Where Unit 313 assignments go wrong

- Describing good practice in general in AC1.2 and AC1.3 without separating the benefit to the organisation from the effect on customers.
- Stopping at developing good practice (LO3) and never saying how it is maintained and what happens if it is not (AC4.2 and AC4.3).
- Forgetting barriers. AC3.2 and AC3.3 ask you to examine them and explain how to overcome them.

## How CMI suggests Unit 313 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Respond to a scenario, task or project based on how you would identify an opportunity to develop, implement, maintain and share good practice in the workplace
- Write a report based on the principles of developing good practice
- Draw upon your own experience of developing good practice within your own area of work

CMI Level 3 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 2,000 to 2,500 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.
