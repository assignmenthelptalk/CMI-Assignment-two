## What CMI Unit 602 covers

According to the CMI Level 6 syllabus (July 2026), Unit 602 is worth 6 credits, with 19 guided learning hours and 60 hours of total unit time. It has 2 learning outcomes and 5 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Development, management, leadership, structure, human resources, theoretical approaches, policy, procedure, regulatory, legal requirements, learning, skills, success.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the principles for developing, managing and leading individuals and teams

- 1.1 **Critically examine** the factors which influence the way individuals and teams are developed and led
- 1.2 **Examine** how organisational frameworks support the management and development of individuals and teams

### Learning outcome 2: Know how to develop, manage and lead individuals and teams to achieve results

- 2.1 **Critically assess** approaches to team development
- 2.2 **Evaluate** approaches to the management and development of individuals
- 2.3 **Critique** the skills required to lead and develop individuals and teams to achieve results

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 602 are:

- **Critically examine** (1.1): examine the subject thoroughly, testing its assumptions, logic and implications, and reach an informed judgement.
- **Examine** (1.2): inspect the subject thoroughly to establish its nature or condition.
- **Critically assess** (2.1): give a reasoned judgement on the arguments made by others and on the evidence behind them, checking the validity of your sources.
- **Evaluate** (2.2): weigh strengths and weaknesses and arguments for and against, then reach a reasoned judgement.
- **Critique** (2.3): give a detailed analysis and assessment of the subject.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 602 assignment

- Use one team and one individual from your own organisation as the case for LO1 and LO2, so the factors in AC1.1 and the approaches in AC2.1 and AC2.2 can be tested against real evidence.
- AC1.1 asks for internal and external factors. The indicative content includes vision, values, culture and structure inside the organisation, as well as external influences.
- AC2.2 is an evaluation. Link goal setting to organisational objectives and KPIs, then weigh how well your chosen performance management tools work.

## Where Unit 602 assignments go wrong

- Retelling Tuckman's stages in AC2.1. The criterion asks you to critically assess approaches to team development, so compare at least two and judge them.
- Describing skills in AC2.3 as a list. A critique weighs how far each skill really develops individuals and teams.
- Covering policies and procedures in AC1.2 without showing how they support management and development.

## How CMI suggests Unit 602 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write an account or assignment on the foundations for developing, managing and leading individuals and teams
- Respond to a scenario based on how you would manage, lead and develop individuals and teams to achieve results
- Present work-based evidence accompanied by reports/reflective accounts to meet each assessment criteria

CMI Level 6 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 4,000 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.
