## What CMI Unit 511 covers

According to the CMI Level 5 syllabus (June 2026), Unit 511 is worth 5 credits, with 21 guided learning hours and 50 hours of total unit time. It has 4 learning outcomes and 11 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Legislation, operational requirements, labour markets, business case, talent, diverse, on boarding , induction, retention.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the factors that impact on recruitment and selection

- 1.1 **Examine** the impact of regulatory and legislative frameworks on an organisation's approach to recruitment and selection
- 1.2 **Evaluate** organisational factors which impact on recruitment and selection
- 1.3 **Analyse** the impact of labour markets on recruitment and selection

### Learning outcome 2: Know how to plan for recruitment and selection

- 2.1 **Examine** the types and purpose of information required to develop a business case for recruitment
- 2.2 **Analyse** good practice in creating content for job descriptions and person specifications to meet business needs

### Learning outcome 3: Understand approaches to the recruitment and selection of a diverse and talented workforce

- 3.1 **Evaluate** methods for generating high quality applications from prospective employees
- 3.2 **Analyse** the rationale for using recruitment and selection methods in different contexts
- 3.3 **Evaluate** approaches for selecting the successful applicant

### Learning outcome 4: Understand strategies for retaining talent

- 4.1 **Analyse** good practice for onboarding and inducting new staff
- 4.2 **Evaluate** methods for improving employee retention
- 4.3 **Examine** the benefits of employee retention for individuals, teams and organisation

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 511 are:

- **Examine** (1.1, 2.1, 4.3): look closely at the subject and set out what it is, how it works and what it affects.
- **Evaluate** (1.2, 3.1, 3.3, 4.2): weigh strengths against limitations with evidence and reach a supported judgement.
- **Analyse** (1.3, 2.2, 3.2, 4.1): break the subject into parts and show how they relate, including causes and effects, with evidence.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 511 assignment

- Build AC2.1 around a business case for one vacancy, with the information you would need: workload, budget, labour market, risk.
- AC2.2 asks for good practice in job descriptions and person specifications. Quote the structure you would use and show how it avoids bias.
- LO4 is often under-written. Cover induction (AC4.1), retention methods (AC4.2) and the benefits of retention (AC4.3) separately.

## Where Unit 511 assignments go wrong

- Describing legislation in AC1.1 without its effect on recruitment practice.
- Treating AC3.1 and AC3.3 as the same thing: attracting applicants and selecting among them are different tasks.
- Leaving retention as a list of perks.

## How CMI suggests Unit 511 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write an account or assignment on the principles of recruiting, selecting and retaining talent in the workplace
- The learner should draw upon your own experience either from their current work role, paid or voluntary. Recruitment, selection and retention are not constrained by being in a particular role and can include planning for a future role
- If not in employment, learners can draw upon organisations with which they are familiar e.g. a past employer or as part of a case study or scenario

CMI Level 5 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. Each unit has a guideline word count with a 10% allowance above or below, so check the figure in your brief before you plan your sections.

## CMI Unit 511 FAQ

**Is this the same as Unit 512?**

No. Unit 511 is recruiting, selecting and retaining talent. Unit 512 is workforce planning, which is about forecasting and planning headcount and skills.

**Does the unit include onboarding?**

Yes, AC4.1 covers good practice for onboarding and inducting new staff.
