## What CMI Unit 701 covers

According to the CMI Level 7 syllabus (August 2026), Unit 701 is worth 11 credits, with 36 guided learning hours and 110 hours of total unit time. It has 2 learning outcomes and 4 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Strategy, leadership, culture, roles, responsibilities, outcomes, theory, approaches, success.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the role and context for strategic leadership

- 1.1 **Critically appraise** the impact of organisational context on strategic leadership
- 1.2 **Critically appraise** the role of the strategic leader to set and realise organisational goals

### Learning outcome 2: Understand the behaviours and skills for strategic leadership

- 2.1 **Critically appraise** the leadership behaviours and skills required to deliver strategic goals
- 2.2 **Critically reflect** on how the principles of strategic leadership can be applied to respond to complex organisational challenges

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 701 are:

- **Critically appraise** (1.1, 1.2, 2.1): use a systematic process to find the strengths and weaknesses of information and judge how useful and valid it is.
- **Critically reflect** (2.2): question and assess deeply held beliefs and assumptions about your own practice, not just describe it.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 701 assignment

- Unit 701 is the largest Level 7 unit, at 11 credits and a 4,500-word guideline. Plan the word count across all four criteria before you write, so the two reflective and complex-challenge criteria in LO2 are not squeezed.
- AC1.1 and AC1.2 are separate. Appraise how context (purpose, vision, mission, narrative, structure, governance) shapes strategic leadership first, then appraise what the strategic leader actually does to set and realise goals.
- For AC2.2 choose one complex challenge from your own organisation, such as organisational change or shrinking resources, and reflect critically on how strategic leadership principles did or did not help.

## Where Unit 701 assignments go wrong

- Writing a catalogue of leadership theories in AC2.1. The criterion asks you to appraise the behaviours and skills needed to deliver strategic goals, so tie each theory to a goal.
- Treating AC1.1 and AC1.2 as one section. Context and the leader's role are different things to appraise.
- Reflecting on a challenge in AC2.2 without questioning your own assumptions, which is what "critically reflect" asks for.

## How CMI suggests Unit 701 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write a report or deliver a presentation on the role and context of strategic leadership
- Write an account on the behaviours and skills for strategic leadership
- Produce a reflective account on their leadership practice to evidence their ability to meet each of the assessment criteria

CMI Level 7 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 4,500 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.

## CMI Unit 701 FAQ

**Is Unit 701 compulsory at Level 7?**

Unit 701 is a Group A unit. Whether it is required for your qualification depends on the rules of combination for the Award, Certificate, Diploma or Extended Diploma, so check with your centre.

**Do I have to cover every leadership theory the syllabus lists?**

No. CMI says learners are not required to cover all the indicative content. Choose models that suit your organisation and support them with evidence.
