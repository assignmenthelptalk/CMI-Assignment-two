## What CMI Unit 320 covers

According to the CMI Level 3 syllabus (October 2024), Unit 320 is worth 5 credits, with 18 guided learning hours and 50 hours of total unit time. It has 5 learning outcomes and 13 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Presentations, type, purpose, target audience, styles, techniques, resources, planning, delivering, reviewing, engaging, impact.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Know the purpose of presentations and the methods used to meet the needs of a target audience

- 1.1 **Explain** the purpose of presentations within business settings
- 1.2 **Outline** the methods used to deliver presentations to meet the needs of different target audiences

### Learning outcome 2: Understand what is required to be a successful presenter

- 2.1 **Assess** the use of different styles for delivering a presentation to meet the needs of a target audience
- 2.2 **Assess** the skills and abilities of an effective presenter
- 2.3 **Explain** how challenges to delivering presentations can be overcome
- 2.4 **Explain** techniques for overcoming fear and anxiety when delivering a presentation

### Learning outcome 3: Understand how to plan, deliver and review presentations

- 3.1 **Outline** the activities required when planning a presentation
- 3.2 **Compare** methods for reviewing the delivery of a presentation
- 3.3 **Discuss** the range of follow-up activities required after a presentation

### Learning outcome 4: Be able to plan and deliver a presentation to a target audience

- 4.1 **Produce** a plan to deliver a presentation to a target audience
- 4.2 **Deliver** a presentation to a target audience

### Learning outcome 5: Be able to reflect on the outcomes of the delivery of a presentation

- 5.1 **Review** own performance in delivering a presentation
- 5.2 **Identify** opportunities for developing own presentation skills

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 320 are:

- **Explain** (1.1, 2.3, 2.4): say how and why, not just what.
- **Outline** (1.2, 3.1): give the main points briefly.
- **Assess** (2.1, 2.2): weigh up the importance, extent or effect of something and make a judgement.
- **Compare** (3.2): set two or more things side by side and say how they are alike and different.
- **Discuss** (3.3): set out the different arguments or viewpoints and reach a reasoned position.
- **Produce** (4.1): write or prepare the finished item.
- **Deliver** (4.2): carry out the activity itself, here the presentation, to the stated audience.
- **Review** (5.1): look back at what happened and say how well it went and why.
- **Identify** (5.2): name or pick out the relevant items and say why they matter.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 320 assignment

- This unit includes practical criteria: AC4.1 asks you to produce a plan and AC4.2 to deliver a presentation. Allow time for the delivery and check with your centre how it is evidenced.
- Plan for one target audience from the start, so styles (AC2.1), methods (AC1.2) and your plan (AC4.1) all fit it.
- LO5 closes the loop. Use real feedback or an evaluation form for AC5.1 and name specific development opportunities for AC5.2.

## Where Unit 320 assignments go wrong

- Treating the written assignment as the whole unit. Delivery of a presentation is part of the assessment (AC4.2).
- Listing skills in AC2.2 without assessing which matter most for your audience.
- Covering nerves in AC2.4 with general advice. The indicative content points to techniques such as practising, rehearsing, cue cards and visualisation.

## How CMI suggests Unit 320 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Whichever route you take, CMI says you must deliver a presentation as part of this assessment
- Write a report or a staff development guide on how to deliver successful presentations to a target audience, and present that guide as part of the assessment
- Present work-based evidence along with a short presentation. CMI says the presentation need not exceed 15 minutes but must be accompanied by a presentation outline or script and a presentation plan

CMI Level 3 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 2,000 to 2,500 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.

## CMI Unit 320 FAQ

**Do I have to present live for Unit 320?**

AC4.2 asks you to deliver a presentation to a target audience, and the syllabus says it is delivered formally using your presentation plan. Your centre confirms how it is observed or recorded.

**What is the difference between AC4.1 and AC4.2?**

AC4.1 asks you to produce a plan to deliver a presentation, including aims, structure and resources. AC4.2 asks you to deliver the presentation formally, following that plan.
