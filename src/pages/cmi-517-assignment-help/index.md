## What CMI Unit 517 covers

According to the CMI Level 5 syllabus (June 2026), Unit 517 is worth 5 credits, with 23 guided learning hours and 50 hours of total unit time. It has 2 learning outcomes and 8 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Benchmarking, consensus, competitive advantage, market development, product/service development, innovation, collaboration, intellectual property, feedback, empowering, involvement, creativity.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the role of innovation within organisations

- 1.1 **Examine** the reasons for innovation in organisations
- 1.2 **Analyse** the types of innovation within organisations
- 1.3 **Evaluate** factors that support a culture of innovation in organisations

### Learning outcome 2: Understand the process of managing innovation in an organisation

- 2.1 **Evaluate** methods used to drive innovation in an organisation
- 2.2 **Examine** the process for innovation in an organisation
- 2.3 **Discuss** the role of the manager in leading innovation in an organisation
- 2.4 **Analyse** the role of stakeholders in the innovation process
- 2.5 **Evaluate** methods used to measure the impact of innovation in an organisation

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 517 are:

- **Examine** (1.1, 2.2): look closely at the subject and set out what it is, how it works and what it affects.
- **Analyse** (1.2, 2.4): break the subject into parts and show how they relate, including causes and effects, with evidence.
- **Evaluate** (1.3, 2.1, 2.5): weigh strengths against limitations with evidence and reach a supported judgement.
- **Discuss** (2.3): set out the different arguments or viewpoints and reach a reasoned position.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 517 assignment

- Take one innovation (a product, service or process change) and use it to illustrate the reasons, type and process.
- AC1.2 needs types, so classify yours (incremental, radical, disruptive and so on) and explain why.
- AC2.5 asks you to evaluate how the impact of innovation is measured. Pick measures such as revenue, cost, customer feedback or market share, and say what each does and does not show.

## Where Unit 517 assignments go wrong

- Describing creativity techniques without examining the process for innovation (AC2.2).
- Leaving stakeholders out of AC2.4.
- Treating AC1.3 as a list of innovative cultures instead of the factors that support one.

## How CMI suggests Unit 517 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write an account or assignment on the principles of innovation in organisations
- Respond to a scenario based on how you would develop an innovative idea/concept within an organisation
- Write a report based on the principles of developing an innovative idea
- Draw upon your own experience of developing an innovative idea within an organisational setting

CMI Level 5 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. Each unit has a guideline word count with a 10% allowance above or below, so check the figure in your brief before you plan your sections.
