## What CMI Unit 314 covers

According to the CMI Level 3 syllabus (October 2024), Unit 314 is worth 5 credits, with 25 guided learning hours and 50 hours of total unit time. It has 4 learning outcomes and 14 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Managing, monitoring, compliance and governance, resources, value for money, finances, equipment, people.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the importance of managing resources in own area of responsibility

- 1.1 **Explain** types of resources managed within an area of responsibility
- 1.2 **Identify** the importance of managing resources effectively in own area of responsibility
- 1.3 **Explain** methods used to achieve value for money when managing resources

### Learning outcome 2: Understand the use of budgets in an organisation

- 2.1 **Outline** the purpose of a budget
- 2.2 **Explain** the types and use of budgets within an organisation
- 2.3 **Explain** the importance of operating within a budget
- 2.4 **Explain** how organisational governance informs the management of budgets

### Learning outcome 3: Understand how to manage costs within a budget

- 3.1 **Explain** the types of costs within a budget
- 3.2 **Identify** sources of information on costs in a budget
- 3.3 **Identify** the systems used for managing costs within a budget
- 3.4 **Explain** limits of authority when managing costs within a budget

### Learning outcome 4: Know how to monitor and control a budget in own area of responsibility

- 4.1 **Explain** methods for monitoring a budget
- 4.2 **Outline** the actions to take to control a budget
- 4.3 **Identify** a process to escalate problems and changes to a budget in own area of responsibility

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 314 are:

- **Explain** (1.1, 1.3, 2.2, 2.3, 2.4, 3.1, 3.4, 4.1): say how and why, not just what.
- **Identify** (1.2, 3.2, 3.3, 4.3): name or pick out the relevant items and say why they matter.
- **Outline** (2.1, 4.2): give the main points briefly.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## Models and sources the indicative content suggests

The indicative content for Unit 314 points to the following. They are examples, not a required list, but they show what the criteria expect you to draw on:

- Budget types: master, operating, expenses, departmental, functional, capital and cash
- Fixed, variable and semi-variable costs
- Variance analysis, actual versus forecast, cost tracking and trend analysis
- Organisational governance and compliance procedures

## How to approach a Unit 314 assignment

- Work from one real or realistic budget for your area. Name the resources in AC1.1, the type of budget in AC2.2 and the costs in AC3.1.
- For AC4.1 show the monitoring calculation itself, such as actual against forecast and the resulting variance, so it is clear you can do it.
- AC3.4 and AC4.3 are about authority and escalation. State approval levels, who signs off, and how you report a problem.

## Where Unit 314 assignments go wrong

- Explaining budgeting in general in AC2.1, which only asks you to outline the purpose of a budget.
- Naming sources of cost information in AC3.2 and systems in AC3.3 without telling them apart. Sources include invoices and purchase orders, while systems include spreadsheets and accounts software.
- Skipping value for money in AC1.3. The indicative content points to negotiation, discounts, bulk buying and alternative suppliers.

## How CMI suggests Unit 314 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Respond to a scenario based on how you would manage resources, costs and budgets within an organisation to achieve results
- Write a report based on the principles of managing resources, costs and budgets
- Draw upon your own experience of managing resources, costs and budgets within your own organisation
- Present work based evidence to support their answer

CMI Level 3 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 2,000 to 2,500 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.
