## What CMI Unit 523 covers

According to the CMI Level 5 syllabus (June 2026), Unit 523 is worth 5 credits, with 26 guided learning hours and 50 hours of total unit time. It has 3 learning outcomes and 9 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Product and service development, marketing environment, marketing planning, competitiveness, innovation, goals, objectives, key performance indicators, strategies, growth, market share, customer focus, stakeholder engagement, product, price, place, promotion, service marketing.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the role of marketing to support the achievement of organisational objectives

- 1.1 **Evaluate** the role of marketing to support the achievement of organisational objectives

### Learning outcome 2: Understand the factors in the organisation's marketing environment which impact on the marketing of a product or service

- 2.1 **Evaluate** the influence of stakeholders on an organisation's approach to marketing a product or service
- 2.2 **Examine** the impact of an organisation's strengths and weaknesses on the marketing of a product or service
- 2.3 **Examine** the impact of opportunities and threats in the marketing environment on the marketing of a product or service

### Learning outcome 3: Know how to market a product or service

- 3.1 **Recommend** approaches to support the marketing of a product or service
- 3.2 **Develop** a tactical plan to market a product or service using the marketing mix
- 3.3 **Analyse** resource requirements to market a product or service
- 3.4 **Analyse** risks that could impact on the marketing of a product or service
- 3.5 **Examine** methods used to measure marketing outcomes

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 523 are:

- **Evaluate** (1.1, 2.1): weigh strengths against limitations with evidence and reach a supported judgement.
- **Examine** (2.2, 2.3, 3.5): look closely at the subject and set out what it is, how it works and what it affects.
- **Recommend** (3.1): propose actions and give reasons that link back to your findings.
- **Develop** (3.2): produce a plan or approach that would work for a stated need.
- **Analyse** (3.3, 3.4): break the subject into parts and show how they relate, including causes and effects, with evidence.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 523 assignment

- Choose one product or service and keep it constant. The SWOT-style analysis in AC2.2 and AC2.3 should feed the tactical plan in AC3.2.
- AC3.2 names the marketing mix. Cover each element and justify it against your analysis.
- AC3.3 and AC3.4 ask for resources and risks, so give approximate resource needs and at least two real risks with responses.

## Where Unit 523 assignments go wrong

- Listing the 4Ps with no link to the environment analysis.
- Ignoring stakeholder influence (AC2.1).
- Naming measures in AC3.5 with no way to collect them.

## How CMI suggests Unit 523 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write an account or assignment on the principles of marketing products or services
- Respond to a scenario based on how you would develop and market a product or service for their chosen organisation
- Write a report based on the principles of developing and marketing products or services
- Draw upon your own experience of formulating plans to develop and market a product or service within your own organisation

CMI Level 5 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. Each unit has a guideline word count with a 10% allowance above or below, so check the figure in your brief before you plan your sections.

## CMI Unit 523 FAQ

**Does the unit require a full marketing plan?**

AC3.2 asks for a tactical plan using the marketing mix. A full strategic plan is not required.

**Which framework fits AC2.2 and AC2.3?**

SWOT is the obvious fit, and PESTLE can support the external side.
