## What CMI Unit 608 covers

According to the CMI Level 6 syllabus (July 2026), Unit 608 is worth 7 credits, with 20 guided learning hours and 70 hours of total unit time. It has 2 learning outcomes and 5 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Corporate social responsibility, sustainability, enablers, constraints, barriers, theoretical concepts and frameworks, stakeholders, ethics, implementation, communication, performance improvement, success.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand corporate social responsibility and sustainability in organisational contexts

- 1.1 **Discuss** organisational approaches to corporate social responsibility and sustainability
- 1.2 **Critically assess** enablers, constraints and barriers to corporate social responsibility and sustainability
- 1.3 **Present** a case for organisational commitment to corporate social responsibility and sustainability

### Learning outcome 2: Know how corporate social responsibility and sustainability is applied in an organisational setting

- 2.1 **Justify** an approach to leading and implementing corporate social responsibility and sustainability in an organisational context
- 2.2 **Propose** a strategy to gain ongoing stakeholder commitment to corporate social responsibility and sustainability

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 608 are:

- **Discuss** (1.1): give a detailed account that includes a range of views, including contrasting perspectives.
- **Critically assess** (1.2): give a reasoned judgement on the arguments made by others and on the evidence behind them, checking the validity of your sources.
- **Present** (1.3): make the outcome of your work clear to an audience of stakeholders, which may take the form of a presentation.
- **Justify** (2.1): give a rationale for actions or decisions, backed by research, theory, data or experience.
- **Propose** (2.2): put forward a proposal supported by a clear rationale suited to the context.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 608 assignment

- AC1.3 asks you to present a case. Write it as a persuasive argument for one organisation's commitment, citing legal requirements, reputation and customer expectations.
- Treat enablers, constraints and barriers in AC1.2 as separate groups. The indicative content includes structure, culture and workforce demographics.
- Link LO2 to LO1: the approach you justify in AC2.1 should deal with barriers you identified, and the strategy in AC2.2 should keep stakeholders engaged over time.

## Where Unit 608 assignments go wrong

- Treating CSR and sustainability as the same thing without defining either. The indicative content notes that both are contested.
- Presenting a case in AC1.3 that reads as description rather than argument.
- Proposing stakeholder commitment in AC2.2 as a one-off announcement rather than an ongoing strategy.

## How CMI suggests Unit 608 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Be required to produce a written report or develop a presentation on corporate social responsibility and sustainability in organisational contexts
- Be required to develop a proposal on how corporate social responsibility and sustainability is applied in an organisational setting
- Present work-based evidence accompanied by reports/reflective accounts to meet each of the assessment criteria

CMI Level 6 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 3,500 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.
