## What CMI Unit 319 covers

According to the CMI Level 3 syllabus (October 2024), Unit 319 is worth 4 credits, with 19 guided learning hours and 40 hours of total unit time. It has 4 learning outcomes and 11 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Purposes, types, innovative, factors, prepare, information, resources, good practice, motivate, challenges, set, record, monitor.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the purposes and types of meetings

- 1.1 **Explain** the purposes of meetings
- 1.2 **Compare** different types of meetings
- 1.3 **Identify** factors which need to be considered when selecting meeting types

### Learning outcome 2: Understand how to prepare for meetings

- 2.1 **Discuss** how to prepare for meetings
- 2.2 **Explain** information that needs to be provided to participants in advance of meetings
- 2.3 **Identify** resources required to support the delivery of meetings

### Learning outcome 3: Understand how to facilitate and chair meetings

- 3.1 **Explain** how to chair formal meetings
- 3.2 **Discuss** how to respond to challenges when chairing meetings
- 3.3 **Investigate** innovative ways to facilitate meetings to achieve outcomes

### Learning outcome 4: Understand how to record and monitor meeting outcomes

- 4.1 **Outline** how to record meeting actions
- 4.2 **Explain** how to monitor meeting outcomes

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 319 are:

- **Explain** (1.1, 2.2, 3.1, 4.2): say how and why, not just what.
- **Compare** (1.2): set two or more things side by side and say how they are alike and different.
- **Identify** (1.3, 2.3): name or pick out the relevant items and say why they matter.
- **Discuss** (2.1, 3.2): set out the different arguments or viewpoints and reach a reasoned position.
- **Investigate** (3.3): look into the subject and report what you find, with evidence.
- **Outline** (4.1): give the main points briefly.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## Models and sources the indicative content suggests

The indicative content for Unit 319 points to the following. They are examples, not a required list, but they show what the criteria expect you to draw on:

- Formal and informal, face-to-face and virtual meeting types
- Agendas, minutes and action logs
- Chairing: objectives, timekeeping, inclusivity and agreement
- Innovative facilitation methods such as visual problem solving and open spaces

## How to approach a Unit 319 assignment

- Take one type of meeting you run or attend and follow it from purpose (AC1.1) through preparation (LO2) to outcomes (LO4).
- AC1.2 asks you to compare types, so set at least two against each other, for example a formal committee against a virtual team briefing.
- In AC3.3 investigate a way to run meetings differently, such as exercise breaks or timed responses, and say what outcome it could improve.

## Where Unit 319 assignments go wrong

- Writing how to prepare in AC2.1 and what to send beforehand in AC2.2 as the same list. AC2.2 is specifically information provided to participants.
- Leaving out how you would keep to the agenda and to time, or defuse conflict, in AC3.2. These are the responses the indicative content names.
- Describing minutes only in AC4.1. The indicative content points to actions, responsibilities, deadlines and action logs.

## How CMI suggests Unit 319 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Respond to a scenario based on how you would manage and monitor meetings
- Write a report based on the principles of managing and monitoring meetings
- Draw upon your own experience of managing and monitoring meetings within your own organisation

CMI Level 3 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 2,000 to 2,500 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.

## CMI Unit 319 FAQ

**Do I need to submit minutes or an agenda?**

The criteria ask you to explain and outline, not to produce documents. Your centre's brief may ask for examples as evidence.

**Are virtual meetings included?**

Yes, the indicative content for AC1.2 includes virtual meetings such as video conferencing.
