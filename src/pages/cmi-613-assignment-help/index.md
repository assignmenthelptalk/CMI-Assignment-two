## What CMI Unit 613 covers

According to the CMI Level 6 syllabus (July 2026), Unit 613 is worth 7 credits, with 26 guided learning hours and 70 hours of total unit time. It has 2 learning outcomes and 5 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Equality, diversity, inclusion, benchmarking, improvement, change, culture, legal and regulatory requirements, data and information, benchmarking, strategy, decision making.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the context for leading equality, diversity and inclusion

- 1.1 **Critically evaluate** the opportunities and challenges of leading equality, diversity and inclusion in an organisational context
- 1.2 **Discuss** the legal and regulatory requirements for equality, diversity and inclusion in an organisational context

### Learning outcome 2: Know how to lead equality, diversity and inclusion in an organisational context

- 2.1 **Critically assess** how leaders can apply legal and regulatory requirements for equality, diversity and inclusion in an operational context
- 2.2 **Critically examine** approaches to leading a sustainable culture of equality, diversity and inclusion in an organisational context
- 2.3 **Reflect** on how the principles of equality, diversity and inclusion can be applied by leaders to make an ethical decision

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 613 are:

- **Critically evaluate** (1.1): weigh strengths and weaknesses and arguments for and against, then reach a reasoned judgement supported by current research or theory.
- **Discuss** (1.2): give a detailed account that includes a range of views, including contrasting perspectives.
- **Critically assess** (2.1): give a reasoned judgement on the arguments made by others and on the evidence behind them, checking the validity of your sources.
- **Critically examine** (2.2): examine the subject thoroughly, testing its assumptions, logic and implications, and reach an informed judgement.
- **Reflect** (2.3): consciously contemplate your actions or an issue and give a balanced view.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 613 assignment

- AC1.1 asks you to critically evaluate both opportunities and challenges. Keep them balanced, drawing on reputation, recruitment and customer satisfaction on one side and the challenges of defining and measuring inclusion on the other.
- In AC2.1 show how a leader turns law into practice through policy, data gathering and monitoring, not only that the law exists.
- For AC2.3 choose a realistic situation, such as an allegation of discriminatory behaviour, and reflect on how the principles of equality, diversity and inclusion guide an ethical decision.

## Where Unit 613 assignments go wrong

- Summarising the Equality Act in AC1.2 and not discussing the wider legal and regulatory picture the indicative content describes.
- Writing about leadership styles in AC2.2 without linking them to a sustainable culture of inclusion.
- Treating AC2.3 as a policy statement. The criterion asks you to reflect on applying the principles to an ethical decision.

## How CMI suggests Unit 613 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Written report on the context for leading equality, diversity and inclusion
- Proposal or report on how to lead equality, diversity and inclusion in an organisational context
- Exemplar of decision making which upholds the principles of equality, diversity and inclusion
- Produce a reflective account on the principles and practices of equality, diversity and inclusion to evidence their ability to meet each of the assessment criteria

CMI Level 6 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 4,000 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.

## CMI Unit 613 FAQ

**How does Unit 613 differ from Level 3 Unit 306?**

Unit 306 covers equality, diversity and inclusive working practice at first-line level. Unit 613 asks leaders to evaluate and apply law and lead culture change.

**Which laws does Unit 613 name?**

The indicative content names the Equality Act 2010 and GDPR (2018), among others. The syllabus notes that legislation can change, so use the law that applies to you.
