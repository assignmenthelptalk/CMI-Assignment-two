## What CMI Unit 703 covers

According to the CMI Level 7 syllabus (August 2026), Unit 703 is worth 7 credits, with 18 guided learning hours and 70 hours of total unit time. It has 2 learning outcomes and 4 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Partnership, collaboration, inter-organisational collaboration, intra-organisational collaboration, collaborative leadership.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the role of collaboration and partnerships

- 1.1 **Critically assess** the scope and value of collaboration and partnerships to support the achievement of strategic objectives
- 1.2 **Critically evaluate** the impact of organisational and legal frameworks on collaboration and partnerships
- 1.3 **Appraise** strategies and principles for making collaboration and partnerships a success

### Learning outcome 2: Know how collaboration and partnerships further organisational success

- 2.1 **Present** a rationale for developing a collaborative relationship or partnership to further organisational success

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 703 are:

- **Critically assess** (1.1): give a reasoned judgement on the arguments made by others and on the evidence behind them, checking the validity of your sources.
- **Critically evaluate** (1.2): weigh strengths and weaknesses and arguments for and against, then reach a reasoned judgement supported by current research or theory.
- **Appraise** (1.3): assess the worth, quality or performance of something and form an opinion.
- **Present** (2.1): make the outcome of your work clear to an audience of stakeholders, which may take the form of a presentation.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 703 assignment

- AC1.1 and AC1.2 both carry critical command verbs, "critically assess" and "critically evaluate", while AC1.3 only needs "appraise". Spend the most words where the verb asks for the most judgement.
- For AC1.2 pick the legal and organisational frameworks that apply to your case. The indicative content names the Companies Act, Equality Act, Health and Safety, Prevent and Modern Slavery legislation, so use those that are relevant and show their effect on the partnership.
- AC2.1 is a rationale: identify the opportunity or need, say what the partnership would achieve against the strategic plan, and justify why collaboration is the right route.

## Where Unit 703 assignments go wrong

- Listing types of partnership (public, private, third sector) in AC1.1 without assessing their value for strategic objectives.
- Naming laws in AC1.2 without evaluating how they constrain or enable collaboration.
- Writing AC2.1 as a description of a partnership that already exists, rather than a rationale for developing one.

## How CMI suggests Unit 703 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write a report on the complexities of collaborative relationships and partnerships
- Produce an account of strategies and principles for making collaboration and partnerships a success
- Present a rationale for developing a collaborative relationship or partnership to further the achievement of organisational success

CMI Level 7 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 4,000 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.
