## What CMI Unit 510 covers

According to the CMI Level 5 syllabus (June 2026), Unit 510 is worth 5 credits, with 17 guided learning hours and 50 hours of total unit time. It has 3 learning outcomes and 6 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Types, causes, impact, evidence based judgement, disagreement, resolution.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the types, causes, stages and impact of conflict within organisations

- 1.1 **Analyse** types and causes of conflict within organisations
- 1.2 **Analyse** the stages of conflict
- 1.3 **Assess** the impact of conflict on organisational performance

### Learning outcome 2: Understand how to investigate conflict situations in the workplace

- 2.1 **Discuss** processes for investigating conflict situations in the workplace

### Learning outcome 3: Understand approaches, techniques, knowledge, skills and behaviours for managing conflict

- 3.1 **Analyse** the knowledge, skills and behaviours required to manage conflict effectively
- 3.2 **Evaluate** the use of approaches and techniques to resolve conflict

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 510 are:

- **Analyse** (1.1, 1.2, 3.1): break the subject into parts and show how they relate, including causes and effects, with evidence.
- **Assess** (1.3): weigh up the importance, extent or effect of something and make a judgement.
- **Discuss** (2.1): set out the different arguments or viewpoints and reach a reasoned position.
- **Evaluate** (3.2): weigh strengths against limitations with evidence and reach a supported judgement.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 510 assignment

- Use a real or realistic conflict as a case so that causes (AC1.1), stages (AC1.2) and impact (AC1.3) all describe the same situation.
- AC2.1 is the only criterion in LO2, and it is about the investigation process: how you would gather facts fairly and what you would document.
- For AC3.2, compare two or more resolution approaches against the case, for example negotiation, mediation and escalation, and say which fits and why.

## Where Unit 510 assignments go wrong

- Listing the Thomas-Kilmann modes without applying them to the case.
- Treating AC1.2 as a list of conflict types rather than the stages of conflict.
- Skipping the behavioural side of AC3.1: knowledge, skills and behaviours are all required.

## How CMI suggests Unit 510 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write an account or assignment on the principles of managing conflict in the workplace
- Respond to a scenario based on how you would manage conflict
- Write a report based on the principles of managing conflict

CMI Level 5 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. Each unit has a guideline word count with a 10% allowance above or below, so check the figure in your brief before you plan your sections.
