## What CMI Unit 512 covers

According to the CMI Level 5 syllabus (June 2026), Unit 512 is worth 4 credits, with 16 guided learning hours and 40 hours of total unit time. It has 2 learning outcomes and 6 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Workforce, skills, human resource models, challenges, theory, analysis of data and information, planning.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the rationale for workforce planning

- 1.1 **Analyse** the rationale for workforce planning within organisations
- 1.2 **Assess** challenges which influence workforce planning

### Learning outcome 2: Understand the principles and practices of workforce planning

- 2.1 **Evaluate** the use of theoretical models in workforce planning
- 2.2 **Examine** the use of data and information to determine human resource need
- 2.3 **Examine** the impact of organisational objectives on flexible employment practices
- 2.4 **Develop** a workforce plan to meet organisational objectives

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 512 are:

- **Analyse** (1.1): break the subject into parts and show how they relate, including causes and effects, with evidence.
- **Assess** (1.2): weigh up the importance, extent or effect of something and make a judgement.
- **Evaluate** (2.1): weigh strengths against limitations with evidence and reach a supported judgement.
- **Examine** (2.2, 2.3): look closely at the subject and set out what it is, how it works and what it affects.
- **Develop** (2.4): produce a plan or approach that would work for a stated need.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 512 assignment

- AC2.4 asks you to develop a workforce plan. Do that first in outline, then build the earlier criteria to support it.
- For AC2.1, evaluate at least one theoretical model against your organisation. Say where it helps and where it does not fit.
- Show what data you would use under AC2.2 (turnover, skills inventories, demand forecasts) and what each tells you.

## Where Unit 512 assignments go wrong

- Confusing workforce planning with recruitment. The unit is about the plan and the data behind it.
- Discussing flexible employment in AC2.3 without linking it to organisational objectives.
- Producing a plan without objectives to measure it against.

## How CMI suggests Unit 512 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write an account or assignment on the principles of workforce planning
- The learner should draw upon your own experiences either from their current workplace
- If not in employment, learners can draw upon organisations with which they are familiar e.g. a past employer or as part of a case study or scenario

CMI Level 5 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. Each unit has a guideline word count with a 10% allowance above or below, so check the figure in your brief before you plan your sections.
