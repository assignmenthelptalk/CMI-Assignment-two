## What CMI Unit 713 covers

According to the CMI Level 7 syllabus (August 2026), Unit 713 is worth 7 credits, with 18 guided learning hours and 70 hours of total unit time. It has 2 learning outcomes and 6 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Applied research, evidence-based, research philosophy, action research, research approach, research design, data collection, quantitative and qualitative methods, sampling, ethics, insider research.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the research process

- 1.1 **Critically appraise** the role of applied research in strategic leadership
- 1.2 **Consider** the impact of research philosophies on work-based investigation
- 1.3 **Discuss** the principles of research methodologies and design in an organisational context

### Learning outcome 2: Know how to develop a research proposal of strategic benefit

- 2.1 **Formulate** the research question and aims for a research proposal of strategic benefit to an organisation
- 2.2 **Critically review** a range of literature to inform the research proposal
- 2.3 **Develop** the research design for the proposal

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 713 are:

- **Critically appraise** (1.1): use a systematic process to find the strengths and weaknesses of information and judge how useful and valid it is.
- **Consider** (1.2): take different ideas, perspectives, theories and evidence into account when making a judgement.
- **Discuss** (1.3): give a detailed account that includes a range of views, including contrasting perspectives.
- **Formulate** (2.1): devise or develop an idea or concept in a concise, systematic way.
- **Critically review** (2.2): meet the criterion as worded.
- **Develop** (2.3): elaborate and progress an idea from a starting point, building on the information given.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 713 assignment

- The syllabus names no single author for Unit 713, so your reading list is your own. Use academic sources on research methods and apply them to a real organisational problem.
- AC2.1, AC2.2 and AC2.3 together form one proposal. Write the question and aims first, review literature that bears on them, then design the research to answer the question.
- AC2.3 should cover strategy, methods, tools, data collection, sampling, analysis, time-frames, and ethics and risk, including insider research in the workplace.

## Where Unit 713 assignments go wrong

- Defining ontology and epistemology in AC1.2 without considering how they affect work-based investigation.
- Listing methods in AC1.3 without discussing principles of methodology and design.
- Producing a literature review in AC2.2 that summarises sources instead of critically reviewing them against your research question.

CMI Level 7 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 4,000 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.

## CMI Unit 713 FAQ

**Do I have to carry out the research in Unit 713?**

The criteria ask for a research proposal, not for the research to be carried out. Your proposal should be detailed enough to show it could be done.

**What is the difference between Unit 713 and Unit 712?**

Unit 713 develops a research proposal. Unit 712 develops a business case, plans a project, reports on it, and reflects on leadership.
