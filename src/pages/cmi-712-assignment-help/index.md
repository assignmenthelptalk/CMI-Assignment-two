## What CMI Unit 712 covers

According to the CMI Level 7 syllabus (August 2026), Unit 712 is worth 10 credits, with 24 guided learning hours and 100 hours of total unit time. It has 2 learning outcomes and 5 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Project management methodologies, research methodologies, strategic thinking, planning, project implementation.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Know how to develop a strategic management project

- 1.1 **Develop** the business case for a strategic management project
- 1.2 **Propose** a research design to inform the direction of the strategic management project
- 1.3 **Recommend** a project management methodology and tools to structure project delivery

### Learning outcome 2: Know how to conduct a strategic management project

- 2.1 **Report** on the outcomes of the strategic management project
- 2.2 **Critically reflect** on the leadership skills and behaviours used to conduct the strategic management project

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 712 are:

- **Develop** (1.1): elaborate and progress an idea from a starting point, building on the information given.
- **Propose** (1.2): put forward a proposal supported by a clear rationale suited to the context.
- **Recommend** (1.3): put forward proposals or alternatives, supported by a clear rationale.
- **Report** (2.1): meet the criterion as worded.
- **Critically reflect** (2.2): question and assess deeply held beliefs and assumptions about your own practice, not just describe it.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 712 assignment

- This is a project unit, worth 10 credits with a 4,500-word guideline. CMI's recommendations for assessment include a strategic management project that benefits an organisation, with a project report after it.
- AC1.1 to AC1.3 plan the project (business case, research design, methodology), and AC2.1 reports on a completed project or a defined milestone. Keep one project through all five criteria.
- AC2.2 asks you to critically reflect on your own leadership skills and behaviours during the project. Use a reflective model such as Johns or Schon and Argyris and draw on real moments from the project.

## Where Unit 712 assignments go wrong

- Choosing a project too small to need a business case, research design and governance.
- Reporting activity in AC2.1 instead of findings and recommendations aligned to the project objectives.
- Describing leadership in AC2.2 without reflecting critically on what you would do differently.

## How CMI suggests Unit 712 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Develop a strategic management project which will be of benefit to an organisation
- Develop a project report based on the implementation of the strategic management project
- Present work-based evidence accompanied by reports/reflective accounts to evidence their ability to meet each assessment criteria

CMI Level 7 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 4,500 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.

## CMI Unit 712 FAQ

**Does the project have to be finished for Unit 712?**

The indicative content for AC2.1 refers to an evidenced project report for a completed project or a defined project milestone, so a milestone report can be enough.

**Can Unit 712 and Unit 713 use the same topic?**

Your centre decides this. Unit 713 asks for a research proposal, and Unit 712 asks you to carry out and report on a project, so confirm with the centre how work may be shared.
