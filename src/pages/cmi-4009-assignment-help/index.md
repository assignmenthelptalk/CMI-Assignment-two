## What CMI Unit 4009 covers

According to the CMI Level 4 syllabus (July 2026), Unit 4009 is worth 8 credits, with 40 guided learning hours and 80 hours of total unit time. It has 5 learning outcomes and 10 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Inspection review, Continuous improvement, Review planning, Risk assessment, Affected stakeholders, Progress dashboards, Presentation of findings, Operational changes, Action plans, Workload grading, Job evaluation, Productivity measurement.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the role of a staff inspection review

- 1.1 **Explain** types of staff inspection reviews and the role of the staff inspector
- 1.2 **Discuss** the component parts of a staff inspection review

### Learning outcome 2: Understand how to plan a staff inspection review

- 2.1 **Explain** the planning and conducting of a staff inspection review
- 2.2 **Assess** risk factors associated with a staff inspection review
- 2.3 **Determine** stakeholders impacted by a staff inspection review

### Learning outcome 3: Be able to conduct a staff inspection review

- 3.1 **Prepare** a presentation of staff inspection review findings

### Learning outcome 4: Understand major elements to be considered when determining how work is undertaken and organised

- 4.1 **Describe** major elements to be considered when recommending the need for work to be undertaken in an organisational context
- 4.2 **Identify** major elements to be considered when recommending how work is organised at the operational and organisational levels

### Learning outcome 5: Understand workload grading and job evaluation assessment

- 5.1 **Demonstrate** how to accurately calculate the workload grading for a specific post, from information supplied
- 5.2 **Describe** techniques used in job evaluation which involve weighting factors and common criteria

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 4009 are:

- **Explain** (1.1, 2.1): say how and why, not just what.
- **Discuss** (1.2): set out the different arguments or viewpoints and reach a reasoned position.
- **Assess** (2.2): weigh up the importance, extent or effect of something and make a judgement.
- **Determine** (2.3): work out and state the answer or choice, with reasons.
- **Prepare** (3.1): get the item ready for use, here a presentation.
- **Describe** (4.1, 5.2): give a clear account of the subject, with an example from work.
- **Identify** (4.2): name or pick out the relevant items and say why they matter.
- **Demonstrate** (5.1): show by working it through, here a calculation.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 4009 assignment

- Choose two real reviews or inspections for AC1.1 and explain whether each is regular or periodic, announced or unannounced, and who carries it out.
- LO3 asks you to prepare a presentation of findings, so include the slides or notes. The syllabus accepts annotated notes if no formal presentation exists.
- AC5.1 is a calculation. You are expected to work out a workload grading from supplied information, so show every step. AC5.2 then asks for job evaluation techniques with weighting factors.

## Where Unit 4009 assignments go wrong

- Treating this as a generic audit unit. It is about staff inspection reviews and work organisation.
- Leaving the stakeholders out of AC2.3.
- Describing job evaluation techniques (AC5.2) without covering weighting factors and common criteria.

## How Unit 4009 is assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met and that learners may approach the assessment in several ways. It does not prescribe one format for this unit, so your centre's assessment brief sets the task. It also says you do not have to cover every point of the indicative content: choose well and show you understand each criterion.

CMI Level 4 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 3,000 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.
