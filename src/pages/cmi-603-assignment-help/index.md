## What CMI Unit 603 covers

According to the CMI Level 6 syllabus (July 2026), Unit 603 is worth 5 credits, with 17 guided learning hours and 50 hours of total unit time. It has 2 learning outcomes and 4 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Culture, identity, influence, impact, theory, inter-relationships, leadership, barriers, vision, position, planning, change.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the role of culture within organisational contexts

- 1.1 **Consider** the impact of culture within different organisational contexts
- 1.2 **Critically examine** the factors which influence organisational culture
- 1.3 **Critique** the inter-relationship between culture and leadership

### Learning outcome 2: Understand approaches required to make and sustain cultural change

- 2.1 **Discuss** the approaches to make and sustain cultural change

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 603 are:

- **Consider** (1.1): take different ideas, perspectives, theories and evidence into account when making a judgement.
- **Critically examine** (1.2): examine the subject thoroughly, testing its assumptions, logic and implications, and reach an informed judgement.
- **Critique** (1.3): give a detailed analysis and assessment of the subject.
- **Discuss** (2.1): give a detailed account that includes a range of views, including contrasting perspectives.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 603 assignment

- Analyse one organisation's culture with a recognised model, for example the Cultural Web, and reuse that analysis in AC1.2 and AC2.1.
- AC1.3 asks you to critique the inter-relationship between culture and leadership. Look at how leaders shape culture and how culture constrains leaders, not only one direction.
- For AC2.1 cover both making and sustaining change. The indicative content starts with creating a vision and evaluating the current position, then moves through engagement and embedding.

## Where Unit 603 assignments go wrong

- Stating the impact of culture in AC1.1 without weighing it. AC1.1 says consider, so show your judgement.
- Listing internal factors in AC1.2 but not external ones, or not examining how they interact.
- Presenting a change model for AC2.1 without saying how the change would be sustained.

## How CMI suggests Unit 603 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write a report on the role of culture within organisational contexts
- Write a proposal to make and sustain cultural change in an organisation
- Present work-based evidence accompanied by reports/reflective accounts to meet each of the assessment criteria

CMI Level 6 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 3,500 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.

## CMI Unit 603 FAQ

**Which culture model should I use for Unit 603?**

The syllabus names several, including Handy, Quinn and Cameron, Schein and Johnson et al.'s Cultural Web. You choose the one that suits your case and explain why.

**How is Unit 603 different from Unit 605?**

Unit 603 is about culture and cultural change. Unit 605 is about innovation and change more broadly, including implementation and review.
