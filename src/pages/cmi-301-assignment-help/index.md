## What CMI Unit 301 covers

According to the CMI Level 3 syllabus (October 2024), Unit 301 is worth 7 credits, with 33 guided learning hours and 70 hours of total unit time. It has 4 learning outcomes and 14 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Roles, responsibilities, skills, attributes, behaviours, leadership, management, develop and improve, factors for success, mission, vision, values, governance.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand how organisations operate

- 1.1 **Outline** the governance of different types of organisations
- 1.2 **Explain** the factors which influence an organisation's culture
- 1.3 **Explain** the purpose of organisational mission, vision and value statements
- 1.4 **Outline** the purpose of organisational strategies

### Learning outcome 2: Understand the management role

- 2.1 **Summarise** the role of the manager
- 2.2 **Explain** the role of the manager in communicating organisational strategy to individuals and teams
- 2.3 **Explain** levels of accountability and authority in the management role
- 2.4 **Outline** the legal and organisational frameworks which apply to the management role

### Learning outcome 3: Understand the application of management and leadership approaches

- 3.1 **Explain** the differences between management and leadership
- 3.2 **Summarise** recognised management and leadership approaches
- 3.3 **Explain** how management and leadership approaches can be applied and adapted in different work based situations

### Learning outcome 4: Understand the knowledge, skills and behaviours to be an effective manager

- 4.1 **Explain** the knowledge and skills required to be an effective manager
- 4.2 **Summarise** the behaviours required to be an effective manager
- 4.3 **Explain** the impact of knowledge, skills and behaviours on the team, colleagues and customers

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 301 are:

- **Outline** (1.1, 1.4, 2.4): give the main points briefly.
- **Explain** (1.2, 1.3, 2.2, 2.3, 3.1, 3.3, 4.1, 4.3): say how and why, not just what.
- **Summarise** (2.1, 3.2, 4.2): give the key points in a short, accurate form.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## Models and sources the indicative content suggests

The indicative content for Unit 301 points to the following. They are examples, not a required list, but they show what the criteria expect you to draw on:

- Hersey and Blanchard, Situational Leadership (1969)
- Tannenbaum and Schmidt, Leadership Continuum (1958)
- Lewin, Styles of Leadership (1939)
- Adair, Action-Centred Leadership (1979)
- Blake and Mouton, Managerial Grid (c.1950s)
- Goleman, Emotional Intelligence (1995)

## How to approach a Unit 301 assignment

- Choose one organisation, ideally your own, and use it for LO1 to LO4 so governance, culture, strategy and your own role link together.
- AC1.1 asks for governance of different types of organisations, so outline at least two, for example a limited company and a third-sector body, and say who holds authority in each.
- In LO3 apply the approaches you summarise in AC3.2 to a real situation in AC3.3, such as business as usual against a crisis, rather than describing them in the abstract.

## Where Unit 301 assignments go wrong

- Treating AC1.2 as a definition of culture. The criterion asks for the factors that influence it, such as size, structure, governance and history.
- Listing management and leadership approaches in AC3.2 and never saying how they change with the situation in AC3.3.
- Writing about managers in general in LO4 with no link to the effect on the team, colleagues and customers that AC4.3 asks for.

## How CMI suggests Unit 301 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write a report on the role of a manager within the organisational context
- Develop a short guide which outlines the role of a manager in an organisation
- Draw upon your own experience of being a manager within your own organisation

CMI Level 3 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 2,000 to 2,500 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.
