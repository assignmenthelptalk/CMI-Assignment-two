## What CMI Unit 601 covers

According to the CMI Level 6 syllabus (July 2026), Unit 601 is worth 6 credits, with 20 guided learning hours and 60 hours of total unit time. It has 2 learning outcomes and 4 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Management, leadership, influence, organisational context, reflection, personal values, drivers, skills, communication, collaboration, impact.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the influence of organisational context on management and leadership practice

- 1.1 **Discuss** the influence of organisational context on management and leadership practice with reference to theoretical concepts and contemporary thinking

### Learning outcome 2: Understand the practice of professional management and leadership

- 2.1 **Critically reflect** on values and behaviours which underpin professional management and leadership practice
- 2.2 **Discuss** the knowledge and skills required for a professional manager and leader to deliver successful outcomes for an organisation
- 2.3 **Critically appraise** the use of communication to lead others with impact

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 601 are:

- **Discuss** (1.1, 2.2): give a detailed account that includes a range of views, including contrasting perspectives.
- **Critically reflect** (2.1): question and assess deeply held beliefs and assumptions about your own practice, not just describe it.
- **Critically appraise** (2.3): use a systematic process to find the strengths and weaknesses of information and judge how useful and valid it is.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 601 assignment

- The indicative content for AC1.1 separates theoretical concepts from contemporary thinking. Cover both, for example a classic theory such as Situational Leadership alongside a contemporary one such as Authentic or Distributed Leadership.
- AC2.1 asks you to reflect critically, so write about your own values and behaviours and question them, rather than describing what good values are.
- For AC2.3 appraise one real communication situation, such as a challenging conversation or giving feedback, and judge how well it led others with impact.

## Where Unit 601 assignments go wrong

- Describing organisational context in AC1.1 without saying how it influences management and leadership practice.
- Treating the unit as a list of leadership theories. The criteria ask you to discuss, reflect and appraise, not to summarise.
- Listing communication channels in AC2.3 instead of appraising how communication was used to lead others.

## How CMI suggests Unit 601 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write a report on the influence of an organisation's context on management and leadership practice
- Write an account on the principles which underpin professional management and leadership
- Produce a reflective account on their management and leadership practice to evidence their ability to meet each of the assessment criteria

CMI Level 6 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 3,500 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.

## CMI Unit 601 FAQ

**Is Unit 601 a reflective unit?**

Partly. CMI's recommendations for assessment include a reflective account of your own management and leadership practice, and AC2.1 asks you to critically reflect. Your centre's brief sets the exact task.

**Do I need all the leadership theories the syllabus lists?**

No. CMI says learners are not required to cover all the indicative content. Choose theories that suit your organisation and use them with evidence.
