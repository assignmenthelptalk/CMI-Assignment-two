## What CMI Unit 317 covers

According to the CMI Level 3 syllabus (October 2024), Unit 317 is worth 5 credits, with 19 guided learning hours and 50 hours of total unit time. It has 5 learning outcomes and 16 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Customer service, satisfaction, loyalty, retention, service failure, customer charter, customer delight, service delivery, advocacy, moments of truth, customer experience mapping.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the features and benefits of good customer service

- 1.1 **Explain** the features of good customer service
- 1.2 **Discuss** the benefits to the organisation of delivering a good customer experience

### Learning outcome 2: Understand factors that influence customer needs and expectations

- 2.1 **Identify** the different types of customers served by an organisation
- 2.2 **Outline** the factors which influence the needs and expectations of customers

### Learning outcome 3: Understand the legal and organisational requirements that influence customer service delivery

- 3.1 **Summarise** legal requirements which impact on the delivery of customer service
- 3.2 **Outline** the scope of organisational customer service standards, policies and procedures
- 3.3 **Explain** the consequences of failing to apply standards and legal requirements in customer service delivery

### Learning outcome 4: Know how to deliver a good customer experience

- 4.1 **Outline** the key features of the customer journey
- 4.2 **Discuss** the benefits of understanding the customer journey
- 4.3 **Identify** methods to support teams and individuals to deliver a good customer experience
- 4.4 **Explain** the methods used by organisations to communicate with customers
- 4.5 **Identify** methods for gathering customer feedback

### Learning outcome 5: Know how to identify and respond to customer service problems

- 5.1 **Identify** common causes of customer service problems
- 5.2 **Outline** techniques for responding to customer complaints
- 5.3 **Explain** limits of authority when dealing with customer service issues
- 5.4 **Identify** methods for monitoring customer service delivery

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 317 are:

- **Explain** (1.1, 3.3, 4.4, 5.3): say how and why, not just what.
- **Discuss** (1.2, 4.2): set out the different arguments or viewpoints and reach a reasoned position.
- **Identify** (2.1, 4.3, 4.5, 5.1, 5.4): name or pick out the relevant items and say why they matter.
- **Outline** (2.2, 3.2, 4.1, 5.2): give the main points briefly.
- **Summarise** (3.1): give the key points in a short, accurate form.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 317 assignment

- Choose one organisation and one customer group. Use them for the features of good service (AC1.1), their expectations (AC2.2) and the journey in LO4.
- AC3.1 is about law, AC3.2 is about the organisation's own standards. Keep them in separate paragraphs, then explain what happens if either fails (AC3.3).
- For complaints (AC5.2) describe the steps in order, such as listening, acknowledging, empathising, apologising and agreeing action, and say where your authority stops (AC5.3).

## Where Unit 317 assignments go wrong

- Treating customers as external only in AC2.1. The indicative content includes internal customers such as colleagues, teams and departments.
- Writing the customer journey in AC4.1 as a single transaction rather than the interactions across departments, suppliers and partners.
- Mixing methods of gathering feedback (AC4.5) with methods of monitoring delivery (AC5.4). The criteria are separate.

## How CMI suggests Unit 317 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Complete a report to investigate the nature of customer service, organisational and legal frameworks which impact its delivery and the nature of customer service in a chosen organisation
- Respond to a scenario based on how you would understand the customer journey and delivery of customer service
- Draw upon your own experience using examples from the organisation to demonstrate how customer service is delivered, who is involved in the process and how issues are managed
- Produce a guide outlining good practice in supporting the delivery of customer service at an organisation

CMI Level 3 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 2,000 to 2,500 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.

## CMI Unit 317 FAQ

**Is the customer journey a required model?**

No. AC4.1 asks you to outline the key features of the journey and AC4.2 to discuss the benefits of understanding it. How you present it is up to you.

**Can Unit 317 be written about an internal service team?**

The indicative content for AC2.1 includes internal customers, so an internal service can be a valid focus. Confirm with your centre.
