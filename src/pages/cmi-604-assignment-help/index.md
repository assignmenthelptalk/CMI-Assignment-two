## What CMI Unit 604 covers

According to the CMI Level 6 syllabus (July 2026), Unit 604 is worth 9 credits, with 29 guided learning hours and 90 hours of total unit time. It has 3 learning outcomes and 6 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Programmes, projects, scope, impact, strategy, organisational contexts, influence, approaches, structure, tools and techniques, leadership, success.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand programmes and project management in an organisational context

- 1.1 **Investigate** the scope and impact of programmes and projects within an organisational context
- 1.2 **Critically assess** the challenges of strategic programme and project management

### Learning outcome 2: Understand factors and approaches for delivering a strategy for a programme or project management

- 2.1 **Critically appraise** factors which influence an organisation's strategy for programme and project management
- 2.2 **Critically appraise** the approaches for delivering programme and project management in an organisational context
- 2.3 **Evaluate** tools and techniques used for programme and project management in an organisational context

### Learning outcome 3: Know how to lead programmes and projects to achieve results

- 3.1 **Critically appraise** the professional skills required to lead programmes and projects to success

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 604 are:

- **Investigate** (1.1): carry out a systematic inquiry into the facts to establish the truth.
- **Critically assess** (1.2): give a reasoned judgement on the arguments made by others and on the evidence behind them, checking the validity of your sources.
- **Critically appraise** (2.1, 2.2, 3.1): use a systematic process to find the strengths and weaknesses of information and judge how useful and valid it is.
- **Evaluate** (2.3): weigh strengths and weaknesses and arguments for and against, then reach a reasoned judgement.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## Models and sources the indicative content suggests

The indicative content for Unit 604 points to the following. They are examples, not a required list, but they show what the criteria expect you to draw on:

- PRINCE2, PMI, APM and PMBOK, as approaches to programme and project management
- Waterfall (Royce et al., 1970)
- Gantt Charts (Gantt, 1910)
- Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)
- Programme board and project board structures
- Whitmore, GROW (2002), and Gilbert and Whittleworth, OSCAR (2009), for leading people

## How to approach a Unit 604 assignment

- Keep the difference between a programme and a project in view. The indicative content treats a programme as a range of projects, so use a programme with several projects as your example.
- AC2.2 asks you to appraise approaches such as PRINCE2, PMI, APM and PMBOK. Compare at least two against the same programme before deciding which suits it.
- AC2.3 asks for evaluation of tools and techniques. Pick a few, such as WBS or Gantt charts, and judge what each does well and badly.

## Where Unit 604 assignments go wrong

- Treating a programme as a large project in AC1.1.
- Listing the stages of one methodology in AC2.2 instead of appraising approaches against each other.
- Leaving the professional skills in AC3.1 as general leadership qualities with no link to programme and project delivery.

## How CMI suggests Unit 604 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write an account on programme and project management in organisational contexts
- Produce a management report on how to develop a strategy for programme or project management in an organisational context
- Write an account on how to lead programmes and projects
- Present work-based evidence accompanied by reports/reflective accounts to meet each of the assessment criteria

CMI Level 6 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 4,000 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.
