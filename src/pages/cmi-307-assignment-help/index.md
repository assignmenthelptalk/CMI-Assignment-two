## What CMI Unit 307 covers

According to the CMI Level 3 syllabus (October 2024), Unit 307 is worth 4 credits, with 15 guided learning hours and 40 hours of total unit time. It has 4 learning outcomes and 10 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Team, development, monitor, impact, learning and development, induction, growth.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Know the purpose of developing the knowledge, skills and abilities of individuals and teams

- 1.1 **Explain** the purpose of developing the knowledge, skills and abilities of individuals and the team
- 1.2 **Discuss** the benefits of developing the knowledge, skills and abilities

### Learning outcome 2: Know different types of development opportunities available to support individuals and teams

- 2.1 **Summarise** development opportunities available to individuals and team members
- 2.2 **Explain** the advantages and disadvantages of development opportunities identified
- 2.3 **Explain** how development opportunities can be used to comply with legal and organisational requirements
- 2.4 **Explain** how organisations can support professional and personal development

### Learning outcome 3: Be able to identify and select development opportunities for individuals and teams

- 3.1 **Outline** the methods used to identify the development needs of individuals and teams
- 3.2 **Summarise** the factors to consider when selecting development opportunities for individuals and teams

### Learning outcome 4: Know how to monitor the impact of learning and development activities on individuals and teams

- 4.1 **Compare** ways of monitoring the impact of learning and development activities
- 4.2 **Discuss** the benefits of monitoring the impact of learning and development activities

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 307 are:

- **Explain** (1.1, 2.2, 2.3, 2.4): say how and why, not just what.
- **Discuss** (1.2, 4.2): set out the different arguments or viewpoints and reach a reasoned position.
- **Summarise** (2.1, 3.2): give the key points in a short, accurate form.
- **Outline** (3.1): give the main points briefly.
- **Compare** (4.1): set two or more things side by side and say how they are alike and different.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## Models and sources the indicative content suggests

The indicative content for Unit 307 points to the following. They are examples, not a required list, but they show what the criteria expect you to draw on:

- Training needs analysis, as a way to identify development needs
- Professional development plans and appraisal, as sources of need
- Induction training and formal and informal learning methods, as development opportunities
- Key performance indicators and reviews, for monitoring impact

## How to approach a Unit 307 assignment

- Use one team throughout. Identify its development needs (AC3.1), choose between at least two opportunities (AC3.2) and set out how you would monitor the impact (AC4.1).
- AC2.2 asks for advantages and disadvantages of each opportunity you identify, so give both sides for every option rather than one.
- For AC2.3 link development to something the organisation or law requires, such as a licence to practise, role-specific training or a technical qualification.

## Where Unit 307 assignments go wrong

- Describing training courses without saying why they are right for the person or team, which is what LO3 is about.
- Treating AC2.4 (how organisations can support development) as a wish list. The indicative content points to money, time, access to people and resources, and senior management commitment.
- Monitoring impact (LO4) only by learner satisfaction. The indicative content also points to appraisals, observation, stakeholder feedback and KPIs.

## How CMI suggests Unit 307 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Draw upon your own experience either from their current work role, paid or voluntary as to how they develop the knowledge, skills and abilities of individuals and teams
- If not in employment, learners can be given a scenario from which to show how you would develop the knowledge, skills and abilities of individuals and teams

## CMI Unit 307 FAQ

**What is the difference between Unit 307 and Unit 321?**

Unit 307 is about developing other people and teams. Unit 321 is about your own personal and professional development.

**Do I need to cover every development opportunity in the syllabus?**

No. The indicative content lists examples. CMI says learners should choose well and show they understand each criterion.
