## What CMI Unit 5012 covers

According to the CMI Level 4 syllabus (July 2026), Unit 5012 (regulatory unit number M/504/9035) is at RQF level 5 and is worth 7 credits, with 30 guided learning hours and 70 hours of total unit time. It has 3 learning outcomes and 10 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Ethical leadership, Culture building, Core values, Leadership styles, Interpersonal trust, Professionalism, Style adaptation, Social concerns, Strategic direction, Team development, Commitment building, Performance support.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand an organisation's ethical and value-based approach to leadership

- 1.1 **Evaluate** the impact of the organisation's culture and values on leadership
- 1.2 **Discuss** the impact of organisational specific, legal, regulatory and ethical requirements on leadership demands
- 1.3 **Evaluate** current and emerging social concerns and expectations impacting on leadership in the organisation

### Learning outcome 2: Understand leadership styles

- 2.1 **Evaluate** the relationship between management and leadership
- 2.2 **Evaluate** leadership styles
- 2.3 **Discuss** why leadership styles need to be adapted in different situations

### Learning outcome 3: Understand the leadership skills required to ensure team involvement and achievement of objectives

- 3.1 **Discuss** how to establish a culture of professionalism, mutual trust, respect and support within the team
- 3.2 **Evaluate** the impact of a leader's clear focus in leading the team in the achievement of the team objectives
- 3.3 **Evaluate** how the leader supports and develops understanding of the organisation's direction
- 3.4 **Discuss** how leadership styles are adapted to meet changing needs, and to enable team development and commitment

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 5012 are:

- **Evaluate** (1.1, 1.3, 2.1, 2.2, 3.2, 3.3): weigh strengths against limitations with evidence and reach a supported judgement.
- **Discuss** (1.2, 2.3, 3.1, 3.4): set out the different arguments or viewpoints and reach a reasoned position.

The indicative content adds that when you are asked to evaluate in this unit, you are expected to show that you can critically appraise the subject from a range of different perspectives and from both positive and negative aspects.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## Models and sources the indicative content suggests

The indicative content for Unit 5012 points to the following. They are examples, not a required list, but they show what the criteria expect you to draw on:

- Johnson and Scholes (the cultural web), Edgar Schein, Charles Handy and Roger Harrison, on culture and values
- Colenso's work on high-performing teams, on values and on team culture
- STEEPLE or PESTLE factors, for legal, ethical and social concerns
- Tannenbaum and Schmidt, Fayol and Mintzberg, on management and leadership
- Leadership style theories such as Lewin, Morris, Willocks and Knasel, O'Neill, Kouzes, Posner and Adair
- Hersey and Blanchard's situational leadership
- The Trust/Co-operation matrix and John Adair's action-centred leadership model

## How to approach a Unit 5012 assignment

- Open LO1 with a short explanation of why an organisation's culture and values matter and how they are communicated. The syllabus suggests you could study your own organisation or compare it with one that has a very different culture.
- For AC1.2 and AC1.3, the indicative content points to the legal and ethical (L and E) and social (S) factors in STEEPLE or PESTLE, with real-life examples where possible.
- For LO2, review a range of leadership styles, then discuss the advantages and disadvantages of each in specific situations. Personal examples can strengthen the answer.
- AC2.3 and AC3.4 are about adapting your style. The syllabus suggests looking at your own style, what others think of it and why you adapt it.
- For LO3, show how you would build professionalism, mutual trust, respect and support, and how the leader's focus affects the team's results.

## Where Unit 5012 assignments go wrong

- Describing a theory when the verb is evaluate. The syllabus asks for both positive and negative aspects.
- Relying on one theorist. The indicative content says there is a lot of literature, so be selective and refer to a number of theorists.
- Treating leadership and management as the same thing in AC2.1, which asks you to evaluate the relationship between them.
- Discussing leadership styles in general terms without tying them to specific situations (AC2.3).

## CMI Unit 5012 FAQ

**Is Unit 5012 a Level 4 or a Level 5 unit?**

It has a Level 5 code and is at RQF level 5, but CMI includes it in the Level 4 Diploma in Management and Leadership. It is not in the Level 4 Award or Certificate.

**Is Unit 5012 compulsory?**

No. In the Level 4 Diploma, CMI lists it in Group B, the optional units. Check your centre's rules of combination for your own programme.

**Why do the criteria numbers look different in the CMI document?**

The printed syllabus numbers the criteria under learning outcome 3 as 1.1 to 1.4. We number them 3.1 to 3.4, because the indicative content itself refers to them as 3.1 and 3.3. Follow the numbering on your own brief.
