## What CMI Unit 4007 covers

According to the CMI Level 4 syllabus (July 2026), Unit 4007 is worth 7 credits, with 25 guided learning hours and 70 hours of total unit time. It has 3 learning outcomes and 10 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Interview techniques, Recruitment, Performance appraisal, Disciplinary hearings, Questioning techniques, Information exchange, Active listening, Record keeping, Action plans, Legislative requirements, Organisational policy, Interview closure.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the contexts of different types of interview

- 1.1 **Explain** the objectives of different types of interview
- 1.2 **Describe** the conditions under which types of interviews could be conducted
- 1.3 **Summarise** outcomes which could be expected from different types of interview

### Learning outcome 2: Understand the skills of interview techniques

- 2.1 **Describe** the skills of opening an interview
- 2.2 **Distinguish** between skills required in the giving and receiving of information
- 2.3 **Outline** the skills required to guide and control the interview process
- 2.4 **Explain** requirements for the recording of information given and received

### Learning outcome 3: Understand the summarising, closing and follow up of interviews

- 3.1 **Describe** the techniques and skills required to summarise an interview
- 3.2 **Describe** techniques and skills required to close an interview
- 3.3 **Explain** how to organise and manage the results and agreements from interviews

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 4007 are:

- **Explain** (1.1, 2.4, 3.3): say how and why, not just what.
- **Describe** (1.2, 2.1, 3.1, 3.2): give a clear account of the subject, with an example from work.
- **Summarise** (1.3): give the key points in a short, accurate form.
- **Distinguish** (2.2): show clearly what sets one thing apart from the other.
- **Outline** (2.3): give the main points briefly.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 4007 assignment

- Pick three or four interview types and keep them throughout, for example selection, appraisal and disciplinary, so LO1 and LO2 stay concrete.
- AC2.2 distinguishes giving from receiving information. Show questioning and listening skills separately.
- In LO3 follow the interview to its end: summary (AC3.1), close (AC3.2) and how you manage agreements and records (AC3.3), including confidentiality.

## Where Unit 4007 assignments go wrong

- Writing only about recruitment interviews.
- Describing interview skills as a list of adjectives. The criteria ask for what you do and why.
- Forgetting the recording requirement in AC2.4.

## How Unit 4007 is assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met and that learners may approach the assessment in several ways. It does not prescribe one format for this unit, so your centre's assessment brief sets the task. It also says you do not have to cover every point of the indicative content: choose well and show you understand each criterion.

CMI Level 4 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 3,000 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.
