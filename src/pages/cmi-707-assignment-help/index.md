## What CMI Unit 707 covers

According to the CMI Level 7 syllabus (August 2026), Unit 707 is worth 8 credits, with 26 guided learning hours and 80 hours of total unit time. It has 2 learning outcomes and 4 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Design, development, vision, mission, strategy, change, people, teams, organisations, objectives, success.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand organisational design and development

- 1.1 **Critically assess** the factors which influence organisational design and development
- 1.2 **Critically appraise** approaches to organisational design and development

### Learning outcome 2: Understand how to apply the principles of organisational design and development

- 2.1 **Develop** a proposal using the principles of organisational design and development to enable the achievement of strategic objectives
- 2.2 **Recommend** approaches to ensure sustainable organisational design and development are embedded within organisational strategy

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 707 are:

- **Critically assess** (1.1): give a reasoned judgement on the arguments made by others and on the evidence behind them, checking the validity of your sources.
- **Critically appraise** (1.2): use a systematic process to find the strengths and weaknesses of information and judge how useful and valid it is.
- **Develop** (2.1): elaborate and progress an idea from a starting point, building on the information given.
- **Recommend** (2.2): put forward proposals or alternatives, supported by a clear rationale.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 707 assignment

- LO1 asks you to assess factors and appraise approaches, so use design models as the evidence base and keep returning to your own organisation as the case.
- AC2.1 asks for a proposal that uses design principles to enable strategic objectives. The indicative content says the proposal can be a business plan, strategic plan or report covering the whole organisation or a significant part.
- AC2.2 is about sustainability. Show how design and development are embedded in strategy, for example through strategic planning, goals and objectives, and review cycles.

## Where Unit 707 assignments go wrong

- Presenting a structure chart in AC1.2 without appraising the approach behind it.
- Proposing a redesign in AC2.1 without linking it to a strategic objective.
- Stopping at implementation in AC2.2 and not showing how the design stays embedded.

## How CMI suggests Unit 707 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write a report on organisational design and development
- Develop a proposal for organisational design and development
- Produce a reflective account on their work-based practice to evidence their knowledge and understanding of organisational design and development

CMI Level 7 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 4,000 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.

## CMI Unit 707 FAQ

**Is Unit 707 the same as Unit 705 on change?**

No. Unit 705 is about leading strategic change as a process. Unit 707 is about designing and developing the organisation itself, including structure, roles and processes, though change leadership will often connect the two.

**Do I need a design model such as the Star Model?**

The indicative content lists several. CMI does not require a particular one, so choose models that suit the organisation you are analysing.
