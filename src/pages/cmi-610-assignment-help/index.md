## What CMI Unit 610 covers

According to the CMI Level 6 syllabus (July 2026), Unit 610 is worth 6 credits, with 20 guided learning hours and 60 hours of total unit time. It has 2 learning outcomes and 4 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Policy drivers, rationale, purpose, limitations, inter-relationships, procedures, instructions, flexibility, formulation, implementation, policy cascade, review, ethics, diversity.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the context in which a policy is developed

- 1.1 **Discuss** the context in which policy is developed

### Learning outcome 2: Know how to develop, implement and evaluate policy

- 2.1 **Develop** a policy to achieve a specific organisational aim
- 2.2 **Develop** an implementation strategy and plan to support the introduction of a policy to an operational level
- 2.3 **Recommend** approaches to evaluate the effectiveness of policy at an operational level

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 610 are:

- **Discuss** (1.1): give a detailed account that includes a range of views, including contrasting perspectives.
- **Develop** (2.1, 2.2): elaborate and progress an idea from a starting point, building on the information given.
- **Recommend** (2.3): put forward proposals or alternatives, supported by a clear rationale.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 610 assignment

- LO2 is the practical core. Choose one organisational aim and build the policy for it (AC2.1), the implementation strategy and plan (AC2.2) and the evaluation approach (AC2.3).
- AC2.2 refers to options such as a pilot, soft launch, big bang, phased introduction, prototype or parallel operation. Choose one and justify it.
- For AC1.1 cover internal and external policy drivers, from local up to global influences, and the roles of corporate and functional policy.

## Where Unit 610 assignments go wrong

- Writing about policy in general in AC1.1 rather than the context in which a policy is developed.
- Drafting a policy in AC2.1 with no justification for why it is needed.
- Naming measures in AC2.3 without saying how you would collect and review the data.

## How CMI suggests Unit 610 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write a report on the context in which a policy is developed
- Develop, implement and evaluate a policy of own design
- Present work-based evidence accompanied by reports/reflective accounts to meet each of the assessment criteria

CMI Level 6 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 4,000 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.

## CMI Unit 610 FAQ

**Do I need to write a full policy document for Unit 610?**

AC2.1 asks you to develop a policy to achieve a specific organisational aim, so a policy is part of what you produce. Your centre's brief says how it is evidenced.

**What is a "policy zero"?**

The indicative content describes it as a policy to write policies. It is one possible policy subject, not a requirement.
