## What CMI Unit 515 covers

According to the CMI Level 5 syllabus (June 2026), Unit 515 is worth 6 credits, with 22 guided learning hours and 60 hours of total unit time. It has 4 learning outcomes and 8 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Strategic objectives, operational planning, key performance indicators, continuous improvement, setting targets, contingency planning, deliver operational plan, time management, manage resources, problem solving, decision making, monitoring and measuring outcomes.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the principles of operational planning in an organisation

- 1.1 **Assess** the relationship between an organisation's strategic objectives and operational planning
- 1.2 **Evaluate** the use of approaches to operational planning

### Learning outcome 2: Know how to create an operational plan in line with organisational objectives

- 2.1 **Examine** the impact of legal and organisational factors on an operational plan
- 2.2 **Assess** the ethical requirements that influence an operational plan
- 2.3 **Create** an operational plan in line with organisational objectives

### Learning outcome 3: Know how to manage and lead the delivery of an operational plan

- 3.1 **Assess** methods of managing and leading the delivery of an operational plan
- 3.2 **Examine** techniques for problem solving and decision making

### Learning outcome 4: Know how to monitor and measure the outcome of operational planning

- 4.1 **Evaluate** methods to monitor progress and measure the outcomes of operational plans

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 515 are:

- **Assess** (1.1, 2.2, 3.1): weigh up the importance, extent or effect of something and make a judgement.
- **Evaluate** (1.2, 4.1): weigh strengths against limitations with evidence and reach a supported judgement.
- **Examine** (2.1, 3.2): look closely at the subject and set out what it is, how it works and what it affects.
- **Create** (2.3): produce the plan or document itself, fit for the stated purpose.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 515 assignment

- AC2.3 asks you to create an operational plan. Build a short plan with objectives, activities, owners, resources, timescales and measures, then use it as the thread through the other criteria.
- AC1.1 needs a clear link from a strategic objective down to the departmental plan. Show the cascade.
- AC4.1 should name the indicators and how often they are checked, with what you would do if progress slips.

## Where Unit 515 assignments go wrong

- Writing a strategy discussion instead of an operational plan.
- Leaving ethical requirements (AC2.2) out of the plan.
- Describing problem-solving techniques without showing one in use (AC3.2).

## How CMI suggests Unit 515 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write an account or assignment on the principles of managing and delivering operational plans
- Respond to a scenario or case study based on how you would create and deliver operational plans
- Write a report based on the principles of creating and delivering operational plans
- Draw upon your own experience of creating and delivering operational plans within your own organisation
- To meet the requirements of AC 2.3 (Create an operational plan in line with organisational objectives) the learner is required to create a plan for a minimum of one work area for a period of 3 months

CMI Level 5 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. Each unit has a guideline word count with a 10% allowance above or below, so check the figure in your brief before you plan your sections.

## CMI Unit 515 FAQ

**What is the difference between strategic and operational planning?**

Strategic plans set long-term direction. Operational plans turn that direction into the work a department does over a shorter period. AC1.1 asks how the two relate.

**Do I need to submit a real plan?**

AC2.3 asks you to create an operational plan, so the plan itself is part of the evidence.
