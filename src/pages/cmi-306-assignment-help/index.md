## What CMI Unit 306 covers

According to the CMI Level 3 syllabus (October 2024), Unit 306 is worth 6 credits, with 24 guided learning hours and 60 hours of total unit time. It has 5 learning outcomes and 14 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Equality, diversity, inclusive working, key terminology, legal requirements, principles, behaviours, benefits.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Know the legal and organisational frameworks for inclusivity, equality and diversity

- 1.1 **Define** the key terminology used in the context of equality and diversity
- 1.2 **Summarise** the legal and organisational requirements for equality and diversity
- 1.3 **Discuss** the potential consequences of failing to meet legal and organisational requirements

### Learning outcome 2: Understand equality, diversity and inclusive working practices in own area of responsibility

- 2.1 **Explain** the principles which underpin inclusive working practices
- 2.2 **Outline** the behaviours which promote inclusion in the workplace
- 2.3 **Discuss** the benefits of inclusive working practices

### Learning outcome 3: Know how to support equality, diversity and inclusive working practices within own area of responsibility

- 3.1 **Discuss** how issues which affect inclusive working practices can be identified
- 3.2 **Explain** the types of support that can be given to individuals with diverse needs
- 3.3 **Explain** a process for implementing equality, diversity and inclusive working practices

### Learning outcome 4: Understand how to monitor and manage equality, diversity and inclusive working practices within own area of responsibility

- 4.1 **Identify** methods for monitoring equality, diversity and inclusive working practices
- 4.2 **Summarise** how challenges to implementing equality, diversity and inclusive working practice may be overcome

### Learning outcome 5: Know how to challenge discrimination in own area of responsibility

- 5.1 **Explain** the benefits of challenging discriminatory practices of staff and colleagues
- 5.2 **Discuss** how discriminatory behaviour can be challenged
- 5.3 **Explain** limits of authority when challenging discrimination

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 306 are:

- **Define** (1.1): state exactly what the term means.
- **Summarise** (1.2, 4.2): give the key points in a short, accurate form.
- **Discuss** (1.3, 2.3, 3.1, 5.2): set out the different arguments or viewpoints and reach a reasoned position.
- **Explain** (2.1, 3.2, 3.3, 5.1, 5.3): say how and why, not just what.
- **Outline** (2.2): give the main points briefly.
- **Identify** (4.1): name or pick out the relevant items and say why they matter.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## Models and sources the indicative content suggests

The indicative content for Unit 306 points to the following. They are examples, not a required list, but they show what the criteria expect you to draw on:

- Equality Act 2010 and the protected characteristics
- Direct and indirect discrimination, victimisation, bullying and harassment (key terminology in AC1.1)
- Formal and informal methods of challenge, including disciplinary and grievance procedures (AC5.2)

## How to approach a Unit 306 assignment

- Open with AC1.1 definitions, kept precise, since later criteria depend on them. Define direct and indirect discrimination, less favourable treatment, harassment and victimisation in your own words.
- Anchor LO2 and LO3 in one area of responsibility, as the learning outcomes say, so principles, behaviours and support all come from the same workplace.
- For AC5.3 state the limits of your authority clearly, in terms of job role, level of responsibility and organisational procedures.

## Where Unit 306 assignments go wrong

- Naming the Equality Act 2010 in AC1.2 without covering the protected characteristics or the organisational requirements the criterion also asks for.
- Treating AC3.1 (identifying issues) as a list of possible problems rather than how they would be spotted, for example through observation, a complaint or quality improvement.
- Describing benefits of inclusion in AC2.3 and AC5.1 in the same terms. AC5.1 is about the benefits of challenging discriminatory practice.

## How CMI suggests Unit 306 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- A blended assessment approach could be used requiring you to complete a report on the organisational and legal framework for equality and diversity. The learner could then be introduced to a scenario based on how you would support inclusive working practices in their management role
- Write a report based on the principles of equality, diversity and inclusive working practice. This must be accompanied by a selection of well chosen examples to support recommendations made
- Draw upon your own experience of implementing inclusive working practices and complying with equality and diversity in the workplace
- Write a guide on how to develop inclusive working practices in the workplace

CMI Level 3 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 2,000 to 2,500 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.
