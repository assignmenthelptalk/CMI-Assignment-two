## What CMI Unit 316 covers

According to the CMI Level 3 syllabus (October 2024), Unit 316 is worth 5 credits, with 24 guided learning hours and 50 hours of total unit time. It has 3 learning outcomes and 9 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Monitoring, frameworks, systems, standards, customer, compliance, consistency, tools, techniques, processes, procedures, audit.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the purpose of monitoring quality in the organisational context

- 1.1 **Explain** the reasons and benefits of monitoring quality in the organisation
- 1.2 **Explain** the consequences of failing to monitor quality in the organisation

### Learning outcome 2: Understand the use of quality systems and tools and techniques for monitoring outcomes

- 2.1 **Outline** accredited quality systems and standards used in organisations to monitor and measure quality
- 2.2 **Explain** the internal systems used by organisations to check and measure quality
- 2.3 **Summarise** the tools and techniques used for monitoring and measuring quality and improving outcomes
- 2.4 **Explain** the benefits and challenges of using tools and techniques to monitor and measure quality

### Learning outcome 3: Know how to monitor quality and report on outcomes

- 3.1 **Explain** the process of monitoring quality in the workplace
- 3.2 **Explain** how the outcome of the monitoring activity may be assessed
- 3.3 **Identify** methods for reporting outcomes

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 316 are:

- **Explain** (1.1, 1.2, 2.2, 2.4, 3.1, 3.2): say how and why, not just what.
- **Outline** (2.1): give the main points briefly.
- **Summarise** (2.3): give the key points in a short, accurate form.
- **Identify** (3.3): name or pick out the relevant items and say why they matter.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 316 assignment

- Choose a quality system, internal or accredited, and use it to answer AC2.1 and AC2.2, then pick the tool you would use for AC2.3 and weigh its benefits and challenges in AC2.4.
- Keep LO3 as a process: how you monitor (AC3.1), how you assess the result (AC3.2) and how you report it (AC3.3), with a chart or dashboard as an example.
- AC1.2 is about consequences of not monitoring. Give specific ones, such as complaints, returns, reputational damage and lost sales.

## Where Unit 316 assignments go wrong

- Naming a standard in AC2.1 without outlining how it is used to monitor and measure quality.
- Listing every tool in AC2.3. The criterion asks you to summarise the tools and techniques, so choose and explain a few.
- Forgetting that AC2.4 asks for benefits and challenges together.

## How CMI suggests Unit 316 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Respond to a scenario based on how you would monitor quality in the workplace
- Write a report based on the purpose and practice of monitoring on quality within the organisation
- Produce a guide on how to monitor quality in the workplace
- Draw upon your own experience of monitoring quality in the workplace

CMI Level 3 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 2,000 to 2,500 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.
