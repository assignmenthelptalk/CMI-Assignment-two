## What CMI Unit 526 covers

According to the CMI Level 5 syllabus (June 2026), Unit 526 is worth 8 credits, with 30 guided learning hours and 80 hours of total unit time. It has 3 learning outcomes and 9 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Culture and values, ethical and value-based leadership, external factors, leadership factors, different contexts, empowerment, leadership techniques, team cohesion, methods for inspiring others.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand leadership practice in an organisation

- 1.1 **Examine** leadership practices within organisations
- 1.2 **Evaluate** the use of theoretical approaches to responsible leadership
- 1.3 **Analyse** the impact of internal and external factors on leadership practice

### Learning outcome 2: Understand leadership styles

- 2.1 **Examine** the relationship between management and leadership
- 2.2 **Evaluate** leadership styles

### Learning outcome 3: Understand the impact of leadership within organisations

- 3.1 **Evaluate** how leadership supports the achievement of organisational objectives
- 3.2 **Analyse** the concept of empowerment and trust in leading others
- 3.3 **Examine** techniques for creating a cohesive team
- 3.4 **Assess** the need to adapt leadership approaches to meet changing needs

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 526 are:

- **Examine** (1.1, 2.1, 3.3): look closely at the subject and set out what it is, how it works and what it affects.
- **Evaluate** (1.2, 2.2, 3.1): weigh strengths against limitations with evidence and reach a supported judgement.
- **Analyse** (1.3, 3.2): break the subject into parts and show how they relate, including causes and effects, with evidence.
- **Assess** (3.4): weigh up the importance, extent or effect of something and make a judgement.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 526 assignment

- Use one organisation and one leadership role as your reference throughout.
- AC1.2 asks you to evaluate theoretical approaches to responsible leadership. Choose approaches that fit your case, and weigh strengths and limits.
- AC3.2 and AC3.4 are linked: empowerment and trust, and adapting leadership approach as needs change.

## Where Unit 526 assignments go wrong

- Treating the unit as a theory catalogue.
- Skipping the external factors in AC1.3.
- Describing styles (AC2.2) without evaluating them.

## How CMI suggests Unit 526 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write an account or assignment on approaches to leading others
- Respond to a scenario based on how you would lead others
- Write a report based on the principles of leadership

CMI Level 5 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. Each unit has a guideline word count with a 10% allowance above or below, so check the figure in your brief before you plan your sections.
