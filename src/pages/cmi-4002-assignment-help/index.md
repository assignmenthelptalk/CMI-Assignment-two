## What CMI Unit 4002 covers

According to the CMI Level 4 syllabus (July 2026), Unit 4002 is worth 7 credits, with 25 guided learning hours and 70 hours of total unit time. It has 3 learning outcomes and 7 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Stakeholder mapping, Primary stakeholders, Secondary stakeholders, Expectations management, Service level agreements, Performance indicators, Customer satisfaction, Feedback monitoring, Service delivery, Communication plan, Balancing interests, Stakeholder commitment.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand and know organisational stakeholders

- 1.1 **Differentiate** between relevant organisational stakeholders
- 1.2 **Distinguish** between primary and secondary organisational stakeholders

### Learning outcome 2: Understand primary and secondary stakeholder expectations

- 2.1 **Differentiate** between the expectations of primary and secondary organisational stakeholders
- 2.2 **Identify** monitoring systems for analysing the achievement of primary and secondary organisational stakeholder expectations

### Learning outcome 3: Be able to develop provisions offered to primary and secondary organisational stakeholders

- 3.1 **Identify** provisions offered to primary and secondary organisational stakeholders
- 3.2 **Develop** provisions for a primary or secondary organisational stakeholder that would impact on stakeholder expectations
- 3.3 **Devise** a method which reviews the impact of the developed provisions to the stakeholder

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 4002 are:

- **Differentiate** (1.1, 2.1): show how two things differ and why that matters.
- **Distinguish** (1.2): show clearly what sets one thing apart from the other.
- **Identify** (2.2, 3.1): name or pick out the relevant items and say why they matter.
- **Develop** (3.2): produce a plan or approach that would work for a stated need.
- **Devise** (3.3): work out a method or plan that would suit the situation.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 4002 assignment

- Draw up one table in AC1.2 listing primary and secondary stakeholders for a real organisation, then add a column for their expectations in AC2.1. The syllabus suggests this build-up.
- AC2.2 asks you to identify monitoring systems. Use ones that exist in your organisation, such as customer surveys, supplier reviews, staff surveys or investor reporting.
- In LO3 pick one stakeholder group, develop a provision for it (AC3.2) and devise a way to review its impact (AC3.3). Keep these three criteria on the same group.

## Where Unit 4002 assignments go wrong

- Mixing up "differentiate" (AC1.1) and "distinguish" (AC1.2): the first is between stakeholder types, the second specifically between primary and secondary.
- Giving expectations that are generic. Each group's expectations should be specific to your organisation.
- Developing a provision in AC3.2 without any way to review it (AC3.3).

## CMI Unit 4002 FAQ

**What is the difference between primary and secondary stakeholders?**

Primary stakeholders typically have a direct economic or contractual link, such as customers, employees and suppliers. Secondary stakeholders are affected by or can influence the organisation more indirectly, such as the media or local community. Use a definition from your reading and apply it to your own organisation.

**Can I use my own organisation?**

The syllabus encourages real examples where possible. Your centre's brief says whether that is required.
