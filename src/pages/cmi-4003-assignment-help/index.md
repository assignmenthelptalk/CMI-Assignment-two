## What CMI Unit 4003 covers

According to the CMI Level 4 syllabus (July 2026), Unit 4003 is worth 7 credits, with 30 guided learning hours and 70 hours of total unit time. It has 3 learning outcomes and 9 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Organisational culture, Corporate values, Workplace behaviour, Collaborative environment, Value alignment, Conflict management, Personal actions, Decision-making, Business planning, Recruitment, Performance management, Role modelling.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the concept of culture to an organisation

- 1.1 **Determine** a framework for analysing organisational culture
- 1.2 **Explain** internal and external factors that could influence organisational culture
- 1.3 **Evaluate** the current organisational culture

### Learning outcome 2: Understand the impact of values that underpin individual and organisational performance

- 2.1 **Discuss** the concept of values to an organisation
- 2.2 **Outline** the concept of values to an individual
- 2.3 **Describe** how the manager's personal values can influence interaction with team members

### Learning outcome 3: Understand the relationship between values and behaviour

- 3.1 **Analyse** how organisational values can affect organisational behaviour
- 3.2 **Analyse** how organisational values can affect individual behaviour
- 3.3 **Identify** how individual values can influence the behaviours of a team

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 4003 are:

- **Determine** (1.1): work out and state the answer or choice, with reasons.
- **Explain** (1.2): say how and why, not just what.
- **Evaluate** (1.3): weigh strengths against limitations with evidence and reach a supported judgement.
- **Discuss** (2.1): set out the different arguments or viewpoints and reach a reasoned position.
- **Outline** (2.2): give the main points briefly.
- **Describe** (2.3): give a clear account of the subject, with an example from work.
- **Analyse** (3.1, 3.2): break the subject into parts and show how they relate, including causes and effects, with evidence.
- **Identify** (3.3): name or pick out the relevant items and say why they matter.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## Models and sources the indicative content suggests

The indicative content for Unit 4003 points to the following. They are examples, not a required list, but they show what the criteria expect you to draw on:

- Handy, culture types
- Schein, levels of culture
- Johnson and Scholes, the cultural web
- Hofstede, cultural dimensions
- PESTLE, for external influences on culture

## How to approach a Unit 4003 assignment

- AC1.1 asks you to determine a framework for analysis. Choose one model, such as the cultural web or Schein, and say why it suits your organisation. Use it again in AC1.3 to evaluate the current culture.
- For AC1.2, separate external influences (PESTLE is the syllabus suggestion) from internal ones such as symbols, stories, structures and norms.
- In LO2 and LO3, set the organisation's stated values against actual behaviour. The syllabus suggests giving an example where an organisational value conflicts with a personal one.

## Where Unit 4003 assignments go wrong

- Copying out the organisation's values. The syllabus notes this is unnecessary.
- Evaluating culture (AC1.3) with only positives, or only negatives. Both are expected.
- Staying general about the manager in AC2.3. Say what behaviours you show and their effect on your team.

## How Unit 4003 is assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met and that learners may approach the assessment in several ways. It does not prescribe one format for this unit, so your centre's assessment brief sets the task. It also says you do not have to cover every point of the indicative content: choose well and show you understand each criterion.

CMI Level 4 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 3,000 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.
