## What CMI Unit 711 covers

According to the CMI Level 7 syllabus (August 2026), Unit 711 is worth 9 credits, with 32 guided learning hours and 90 hours of total unit time. It has 2 learning outcomes and 5 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Entrepreneurs, intrapreneurs, social entrepreneurship, international/global contexts, finance, environmental factors, entrepreneurial practice, behavioural competences, managerial competencies, business start-up, commercialisation.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand entrepreneurship in strategic contexts

- 1.1 **Critically appraise** the forms and contexts of entrepreneurship with reference to theoretical concepts and contemporary thinking
- 1.2 **Assess** multi-dimensional issues which impact on entrepreneurial practice in different contexts
- 1.3 **Formulate** approaches to removing barriers to entrepreneurial practice using an evidence-based justification

### Learning outcome 2: Understand the principles of entrepreneurial practice

- 2.1 **Critically appraise** the characteristics and attributes of entrepreneurial leadership
- 2.2 **Propose** a course of action to achieve an entrepreneurial aim using an evidence-based justification

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 711 are:

- **Critically appraise** (1.1, 2.1): use a systematic process to find the strengths and weaknesses of information and judge how useful and valid it is.
- **Assess** (1.2): weigh up the importance, extent or effect of something and make a judgement.
- **Formulate** (1.3): devise or develop an idea or concept in a concise, systematic way.
- **Propose** (2.2): put forward a proposal supported by a clear rationale suited to the context.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 711 assignment

- AC1.1 needs forms and contexts. Cover entrepreneurship, intrapreneurship and social entrepreneurship, and place each in a context such as a new start-up or an existing organisation.
- AC1.3 and AC2.2 both require an evidence-based justification. For the barriers to entrepreneurial practice and the course of action, back each choice with research, not opinion.
- AC2.2 calls for a course of action from idea to delivery. The indicative content covers opportunity identification, feasibility, resourcing, implementation and review, so build the plan in that order.

## Where Unit 711 assignments go wrong

- Describing entrepreneurial heroes in AC1.1 instead of critically appraising forms and contexts with reference to theory.
- Listing barriers in AC1.3 without formulating approaches to remove them.
- Presenting an idea in AC2.2 with no feasibility, finance or commercialisation thinking.

## How CMI suggests Unit 711 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write a report on entrepreneurial practice in different contexts
- Respond to a scenario based on how you would develop an idea from concept to delivery

CMI Level 7 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 4,000 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.

## CMI Unit 711 FAQ

**Do I need to start a business for Unit 711?**

No. The criteria ask you to propose a course of action to achieve an entrepreneurial aim, which can sit in an existing organisation as well as a new venture.

**Does Unit 711 include intrapreneurship?**

Yes. The indicative content lists entrepreneurship, intrapreneurship and social entrepreneurship as forms to consider in AC1.1.
