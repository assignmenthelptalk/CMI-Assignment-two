## What CMI Unit 309 covers

According to the CMI Level 3 syllabus (October 2024), Unit 309 is worth 3 credits, with 13 guided learning hours and 30 hours of total unit time. It has 3 learning outcomes and 8 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Conflict management, causes of conflict, professional behaviour, trust, leading by example, influencing skills.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the nature of conflict in the workplace

- 1.1 **Identify** reasons for conflict in the workplace
- 1.2 **Define** the scope of conflict which may occur in the workplace
- 1.3 **Explain** the impact of conflict in the workplace

### Learning outcome 2: Understand the signs and causes of conflict in the workplace

- 2.1 **Discuss** the signs of conflict in the workplace
- 2.2 **Explain** methods used to investigate causes of conflict in the workplace

### Learning outcome 3: Know how to respond professionally to conflict in the workplace

- 3.1 **Compare** formal and informal methods of responding to conflict
- 3.2 **Discuss** the skills and knowledge required when responding professionally to conflict in the workplace
- 3.3 **Explain** how to repair working relationships following conflict

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 309 are:

- **Identify** (1.1): name or pick out the relevant items and say why they matter.
- **Define** (1.2): state exactly what the term means.
- **Explain** (1.3, 2.2, 3.3): say how and why, not just what.
- **Discuss** (2.1, 3.2): set out the different arguments or viewpoints and reach a reasoned position.
- **Compare** (3.1): set two or more things side by side and say how they are alike and different.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## Models and sources the indicative content suggests

The indicative content for Unit 309 points to the following. They are examples, not a required list, but they show what the criteria expect you to draw on:

- Berne, Transactional Analysis (c.1950)
- Thomas-Kilmann Conflict Mode Instrument (1974), cited with a five-step conflict management process
- ACAS (2017), for conciliation and arbitration
- Goleman, Emotional Intelligence (1995)

## How to approach a Unit 309 assignment

- Keep one workplace conflict in mind from LO1 to LO3, such as a dispute over resources or information, and trace it from reason (AC1.1) to signs (AC2.1) to response (AC3.1).
- AC3.1 is a comparison, so set informal methods (listening, questioning, influencing, negotiating) against formal ones (policies and procedures, mediation, conciliation) and say when each is suitable.
- AC3.2 includes knowing when to escalate. Say where your authority ends and who you would involve.

## Where Unit 309 assignments go wrong

- Treating conflict as always harmful in AC1.3. The indicative content says the impact can be positive or negative.
- Listing signs in AC2.1 without discussing them, and not separating signs (AC2.1) from the methods used to investigate causes (AC2.2).
- Ignoring AC3.3. Repairing relationships, through respect, trust, communication and clarifying roles, is assessed as its own criterion.

## How CMI suggests Unit 309 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Respond to a scenario based on how you would respond to conflict in an organisation
- Write a report based on the principles of responding to conflict in an organisation
- Draw upon your own experience of responding to conflict within your own organisation

CMI Level 3 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 2,000 to 2,500 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.
