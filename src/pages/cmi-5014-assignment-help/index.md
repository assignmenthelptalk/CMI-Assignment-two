## What CMI Unit 5014 covers

According to the CMI Level 4 syllabus (July 2026), Unit 5014 (regulatory unit number F/504/9055) is at RQF level 5 and is worth 6 credits, with 60 hours of total unit time. It has 4 learning outcomes and 10 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Workplace coaching, Workplace mentoring, Performance management, Employee development, Employee life cycle, Team learning, Line management, Personal growth, Strategic goals, Role boundaries, Senior management, Development culture.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the concept and purpose of management coaching and mentoring

- 1.1 **Describe** the purpose of coaching in human resources development
- 1.2 **Describe** the role of mentoring in management
- 1.3 **Evaluate** the difference between coaching and mentoring

### Learning outcome 2: Be able to determine the use of management coaching as a tool in human resources development

- 2.1 **Evaluate** the benefits of coaching in performance management
- 2.2 **Explain** the role of coaching in team learning

### Learning outcome 3: Be able to determine the use of management mentoring as a tool in human resources development

- 3.1 **Evaluate** the benefits of mentoring in performance management
- 3.2 **Explain** the role of mentoring in team learning

### Learning outcome 4: Understand the relationship between coaching and mentoring and organisational objectives

- 4.1 **Analyse** the role of a manager as a coach
- 4.2 **Analyse** the role of a manager as a mentor
- 4.3 **Explain** how coaching and mentoring is linked to organisational objectives

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 5014 are:

- **Describe** (1.1, 1.2): give a clear account of the subject, with an example from work.
- **Evaluate** (1.3, 2.1, 3.1): weigh strengths against limitations with evidence and reach a supported judgement.
- **Explain** (2.2, 3.2, 4.3): say how and why, not just what.
- **Analyse** (4.1, 4.2): break the subject into parts and show how they relate, including causes and effects, with evidence.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## Models and sources the indicative content suggests

The indicative content for Unit 5014 points to the following. They are examples, not a required list, but they show what the criteria expect you to draw on:

- Parsloe, Bluckert and Gallwey, on the behavioural aspect of coaching
- Prochaska, Zeus and Skiffington, and Goldsmith, as coaching models
- The employee life cycle, from induction and managing performance to career change and redundancy support
- Witherspoon's coaching continuum, for coaching in a team setting
- A template comparing the role of a manager with the role of a mentor

## How to approach a Unit 5014 assignment

- Keep coaching and mentoring clearly apart throughout. The indicative content says it is important to distinguish between the two processes.
- AC1.3 asks you to evaluate the difference. The syllabus suggests comparing and contrasting them, appraising the differences critically and stating why you would choose one method over the other. A template may help.
- LO2 and LO3 are paired. Use the same structure for each, so the benefits in performance management and the role in team learning are easy to compare. The indicative content for LO3 links back to AC1.3 and AC2.1.
- AC4.1 and AC4.2 can include the advantages and disadvantages of a manager acting as a coach or mentor. The syllabus also suggests asking whether it is appropriate for a line manager to be a mentor, or whether the mentor should come from a different part of the organisation.
- AC4.3 is about the business plan, the team or department plan and individual development plans, and how senior management support and commitment secure buy-in.

## Where Unit 5014 assignments go wrong

- Blurring coaching and mentoring so that the difference in AC1.3 is never made clear.
- Repeating the coaching answer for mentoring without saying when you would choose mentoring instead (LO3).
- Describing the manager's role in LO4 when the verb is analyse.
- Writing about coaching and mentoring in general terms with no link to organisational objectives (AC4.3).

## CMI Unit 5014 FAQ

**Is Unit 5014 a Level 4 or a Level 5 unit?**

It has a Level 5 code and is at RQF level 5, but CMI includes it in the Level 4 Diploma in Management and Leadership. It is not in the Level 4 Award or Certificate.

**Is Unit 5014 compulsory?**

No. In the Level 4 Diploma, CMI lists it in Group B, the optional units. Check your centre's rules of combination for your own programme.

**What is the difference between coaching and mentoring in this unit?**

The syllabus contrasts the results-driven focus of a line manager acting as a coach with the personal growth focus of a mentor. AC1.3 asks you to evaluate that difference.
