## What CMI Unit 509 covers

According to the CMI Level 5 syllabus (June 2026), Unit 509 is worth 4 credits, with 18 guided learning hours and 40 hours of total unit time. It has 4 learning outcomes and 8 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Stakeholder relationships, benefits and challenges, contractual framework, process, planning engagement, role of the manager, communication skills, collaborative working techniques, strategies for managing conflict, impact of stakeholder engagement.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the different types and value of stakeholder relationships

- 1.1 **Analyse** the types of stakeholder relationships within organisations
- 1.2 **Examine** the benefits and challenges for organisations working with different stakeholder groups

### Learning outcome 2: Understand the frameworks for stakeholder management

- 2.1 **Examine** the contractual frameworks for stakeholder engagement and management
- 2.2 **Specify** a process for planning stakeholder engagement

### Learning outcome 3: Know how to manage stakeholder relationships

- 3.1 **Analyse** the role of the manager in managing stakeholder relationships
- 3.2 **Evaluate** the use of collaborative working techniques to manage stakeholder relationships
- 3.3 **Discuss** methods for managing conflict in stakeholder engagement

### Learning outcome 4: Know methods for measuring the impact of stakeholder engagement on organisational performance

- 4.1 **Examine** methods for measuring the impact of stakeholder engagement on organisational performance

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 509 are:

- **Analyse** (1.1, 3.1): break the subject into parts and show how they relate, including causes and effects, with evidence.
- **Examine** (1.2, 2.1, 4.1): look closely at the subject and set out what it is, how it works and what it affects.
- **Specify** (2.2): state clearly and precisely what is required, including the process or reasons.
- **Evaluate** (3.2): weigh strengths against limitations with evidence and reach a supported judgement.
- **Discuss** (3.3): set out the different arguments or viewpoints and reach a reasoned position.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 509 assignment

- Use a stakeholder map for AC1.1 and AC2.2. The syllabus names power and interest approaches (Freeman; Eden and Ackermann), so apply one to a real set of stakeholders.
- AC2.1 is specific: contractual frameworks such as service level agreements, contracts, compliance and governance. Use documents you actually work with.
- Close with AC4.1: a measurement approach for the effect of engagement on performance, with indicators you could really collect.

## Where Unit 509 assignments go wrong

- Confusing the stakeholder relationship types in AC1.1 with a list of stakeholders.
- Writing AC2.2 as a general approach rather than a process for planning engagement.
- Leaving conflict (AC3.3) as a generic list of styles.

## How CMI suggests Unit 509 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Write an account or assignment on the principles of building and managing stakeholder relationships
- Respond to a scenario based on how you would build and manage stakeholder relationships
- Write a report based on the principles of building and managing stakeholder relationships
- For AC2.1 the learner may use work based evidence such as contracts and agreements used within the organisation to underpin their discussion/evaluation for this assessment criterion
- Draw upon your own experience of building and managing stakeholder relationships within your own organisation

CMI Level 5 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. Each unit has a guideline word count with a 10% allowance above or below, so check the figure in your brief before you plan your sections.
