## What CMI Unit 308 covers

According to the CMI Level 3 syllabus (October 2024), Unit 308 is worth 5 credits, with 25 guided learning hours and 50 hours of total unit time. It has 6 learning outcomes and 13 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Benefits, roles, responsibilities, recruitment and selection, planning, monitoring, legal frameworks, volunteer agreements support, motivation, third sector, non-profit, charity.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the reasons and benefits of volunteering

- 1.1 **Explain** the reasons why an organisation engages with volunteers
- 1.2 **Summarise** the benefits an individual may experience in the volunteer role

### Learning outcome 2: Understand the legal and organisational requirements for managing volunteers

- 2.1 **Outline** the legal and organisational requirements for managing volunteers
- 2.2 **Explain** the purpose of a volunteer agreement

### Learning outcome 3: Know how to recruit, select and manage learning and development for volunteers

- 3.1 **Explain** the process for recruiting and selecting volunteers to meet the organisational needs
- 3.2 **Explain** the key features of a volunteer induction programme
- 3.3 **Outline** the purpose of learning and development opportunities for volunteers

### Learning outcome 4: Know how to manage volunteers in the workplace

- 4.1 **Explain** how to plan work activities for volunteers
- 4.2 **Discuss** methods for monitoring the work of volunteers
- 4.3 **Explain** how to respond to problems which occur when managing volunteers

### Learning outcome 5: Know how to build and maintain relationships with volunteers

- 5.1 **Explain** the skills and attributes required to build and maintain relationships with volunteers

### Learning outcome 6: Know how to motivate and retain volunteers

- 6.1 **Explain** the reasons why volunteers may not be motivated to keep their commitment
- 6.2 **Identify** methods of motivating volunteers

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 308 are:

- **Explain** (1.1, 2.2, 3.1, 3.2, 4.1, 4.3, 5.1, 6.1): say how and why, not just what.
- **Summarise** (1.2): give the key points in a short, accurate form.
- **Outline** (2.1, 3.3): give the main points briefly.
- **Discuss** (4.2): set out the different arguments or viewpoints and reach a reasoned position.
- **Identify** (6.2): name or pick out the relevant items and say why they matter.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## How to approach a Unit 308 assignment

- Base the whole assignment on one organisation that uses volunteers, real or realistic, so recruitment (LO3), planning (LO4) and motivation (LO6) fit together.
- Remember the unit has six learning outcomes. LO5 (relationships) and LO6 (motivation and retention) are short but each criterion still has to be met.
- For AC4.3 use the problems the indicative content names, such as conduct, overstepping role boundaries, complaints and timekeeping, and say how you would respond to each.

## Where Unit 308 assignments go wrong

- Writing about employed staff in AC3.1 and AC4.1. The criteria are about recruiting and planning work for volunteers.
- Explaining why volunteers may leave (AC6.1) without the separate list of methods to motivate them (AC6.2).
- Covering the volunteer agreement (AC2.2) as a legal contract. The indicative content frames its purpose as expectations, roles and responsibilities.

## How CMI suggests Unit 308 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Respond to a scenario based on how you would introduce volunteers into an organisation and manage them to achieve results
- Write a report based on the principles of managing volunteers

CMI Level 3 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 2,000 to 2,500 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.
