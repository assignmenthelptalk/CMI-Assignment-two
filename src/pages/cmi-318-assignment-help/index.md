## What CMI Unit 318 covers

According to the CMI Level 3 syllabus (October 2024), Unit 318 is worth 5 credits, with 21 guided learning hours and 50 hours of total unit time. It has 4 learning outcomes and 12 assessment criteria, and every criterion has to be met for the unit to be passed.

Keywords the syllabus lists for the unit: Data and information gathering, qualitative and quantitative data and information, primary and secondary sources, organising and analysing data and information, report writing.

## Learning outcomes and assessment criteria

### Learning outcome 1: Understand the reasons for collecting data and information

- 1.1 **Explain** reasons why organisations collect data and information

### Learning outcome 2: Know the types and use of data and information used by organisations

- 2.1 **Explain** the differences between data and information
- 2.2 **Discuss** sources of primary and secondary data and information
- 2.3 **Explain** types and use of quantitative and qualitative data and information

### Learning outcome 3: Know how to manage data and information

- 3.1 **Explain** the legal and organisational requirements for managing data and information
- 3.2 **Identify** organisational roles and responsibilities for managing data and information
- 3.3 **Outline** approaches for checking data and information against organisational needs
- 3.4 **Discuss** methods used to organise data and information
- 3.5 **Outline** challenges of managing data and information
- 3.6 **Identify** solutions to address key challenges of managing data and information

### Learning outcome 4: Know how to interpret data and information and communicate findings

- 4.1 **Summarise** methods for examining and interpreting data and information
- 4.2 **Explain** the advantages and disadvantages of tools used to communicate findings to a target audience

## What the command verbs ask for in this unit

The first word of each criterion tells you the depth required. The verbs used in Unit 318 are:

- **Explain** (1.1, 2.1, 2.3, 3.1, 4.2): say how and why, not just what.
- **Discuss** (2.2, 3.4): set out the different arguments or viewpoints and reach a reasoned position.
- **Identify** (3.2, 3.6): name or pick out the relevant items and say why they matter.
- **Outline** (3.3, 3.5): give the main points briefly.
- **Summarise** (4.1): give the key points in a short, accurate form.

These are plain-English readings of the verbs. Your centre's assessment brief and its assessor have the final say on what counts as a pass.

## Models and sources the indicative content suggests

The indicative content for Unit 318 points to the following. They are examples, not a required list, but they show what the criteria expect you to draw on:

- Ackoff, DIKW model (1989): data, information, knowledge, wisdom
- Data Protection Act 1998, General Data Protection Regulation, Freedom of Information Act 2000 and Digital Economy Act 2017, named for AC3.1
- Data controller and data processor roles, for AC3.2
- Accuracy, validity, currency, reliability, authenticity and completeness, as checks (AC3.3)

## How to approach a Unit 318 assignment

- Use one data set from your workplace, such as customer feedback, and carry it through LO2 to LO4: its type, how you check it, how you organise it and how you report it.
- LO3 has six criteria. Give each its own short section so none are missed, in particular AC3.5 (challenges) and AC3.6 (solutions) as a pair.
- In AC4.2 weigh the tools you use to communicate findings, such as reports or dashboards, for advantages and disadvantages with a named audience.

## Where Unit 318 assignments go wrong

- Treating data and information as the same in AC2.1. The indicative content separates structured and unstructured material, facts and figures, and context.
- Reciting data protection law in AC3.1 with no link to organisational requirements, which the criterion also asks for.
- Giving only one source in AC2.2. The criterion asks you to discuss sources of both primary and secondary data.

## How CMI suggests Unit 318 can be assessed

CMI says all assessment criteria must be met, and that the following ways of assessing the unit are guidance only. Your centre chooses the format, so follow your own brief:

- Respond to a scenario based on analysing data or information for a specific business issue, problem or solution
- Write a report based on the principles of managing and reporting on data and information
- Draw upon your own experience of managing, analysing and reporting on data and information within your own organisation
- Create a presentation based on principles of managing and reporting on data and information

CMI Level 3 qualifications are graded Pass or Refer. The guideline word count for this unit is 2,000 to 2,500 words, with a 10% allowance above or below, so plan your sections around that.

## CMI Unit 318 FAQ

**Does Unit 318 need statistics?**

The syllabus asks about quantitative and qualitative data and about interpreting findings (AC4.1). It does not set a statistical method, so use what suits your data.

**Which laws does Unit 318 refer to?**

The indicative content for AC3.1 names several data and information laws. The syllabus notes that legislation may change, so use what currently applies.
