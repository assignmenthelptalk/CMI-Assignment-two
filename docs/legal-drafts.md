## 3. Academic Integrity and Disclaimer

**Not affiliated with the Chartered Management Institute.** cmiassignmenthelp.uk.com is an independent service. "CMI" and "Chartered Management Institute" are used only to describe the qualifications our guidance relates to.

### Our position
Everything we provide, including guides, model answers, structured examples and written work, is intended as study and reference material to help you understand a unit, plan an assignment and improve your own writing. It is not intended to be submitted as your own work.

### Your responsibility
CMI centres and awarding bodies have rules on authorship, referencing and plagiarism. You are responsible for knowing and following the rules of your centre and CMI. Submitting work that isn't your own, or that you haven't properly referenced, may lead to penalties, including a refer or removal from the programme.

### How we suggest you use our materials
- Use them to understand the unit and its assessment criteria.
- Use them to see how an assignment can be structured and how sources can be referenced.
- Write your own assignment in your own words, applying your own workplace experience.
- Reference any source you use, including ours if you quote or adapt from it.

### What we don't do
We don't guarantee grades, results or that work will pass any similarity-checking system. We don't advise on getting round centre or awarding body checks.

### Questions
If you're unsure how to use our materials within your centre's rules, ask your centre's assessor or tutor first, or contact us at [email].
